## 5. Primary assignment and boundaries

- Technical sport and trekking footwear stays in Ayakkabı; the sport/activity is a typed facet or the L3 above. Non-footwear equipment stays in Spor & Outdoor.
- Baby and child footwear stays here; age, size system and development stage are facets. Baby clothing stays in Giyim & Moda and baby-care products in Anne & Bebek.
- Ordinary professional footwear stays here. Certified protection-first safety footwear, including certified toe-protection boots, routes to `Yapı, Hırdavat & Tesisat → İş Güvenliği & Koruyucu Donanım`; certification is not a merchandising adjective.
- Comfort insoles stay here only without diagnostic, treatment, rehabilitation or orthosis claims; those claims route to Sağlık & Medikal review.
- Costume-only foot props route to Hediyelik & Parti; normally wearable shoes stay here.

## 6. Facet and synonym hints

Facets: intended wearer, age stage, size system, fit/width, color, material, closure, heel height, sole type, sport/activity, terrain, season, water resistance and evidenced safety standard. Brand and model are never category nodes.
