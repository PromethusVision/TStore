# Bug Reproduction Model

**State:** PROPOSED

## Required fields

- environment/project identity and data class;
- app version/build, commit/artifact hash, platform/device/OS;
- account role and synthetic fixture locator;
- preconditions and clean/upgrade/session state;
- numbered minimal steps;
- actual and expected behavior;
- frequency and first/last observed time;
- safe logs, screenshots/video, trace/correlation ID;
- reproduction certainty: CONFIRMED, INTERMITTENT, INFERRED or NOT_REPRODUCED;
- network/location/lifecycle conditions;
- cleanup and privacy redaction.

Do not request passwords, tokens, private messages or unnecessary customer PII. A non-reproduced report remains valid evidence and may need telemetry or controlled variation rather than closure.

Expected behavior links to a canonical contract or explicit owner decision.
