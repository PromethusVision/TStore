# EsnaftaVar Esenler Commercial Pilot — Product Contract

**State:** `PROPOSED PILOT CONTRACT — OWNER DECISION REQUIRED`

## Mission

Test whether people in a tightly bounded Esenler area can reliably discover a
useful nearby product, identify a truthful physical seller, visit or contact that
shop, and—where enabled—create replay-safe evidence of an in-person purchase. At
the same time, test whether merchants can keep a small useful catalog accurate at
an operational cost the pilot team can sustain.

## Success is

- repeated completion of useful local discovery tasks, not raw installs;
- enough active, truthful supply inside the promised launch cell;
- merchant ability to maintain listing price/availability/freshness;
- safe QR confirmation with explainable exceptions where QR is enabled;
- customer and merchant trust maintained through visible boundaries and support;
- critical incidents observable, owned and recoverable;
- learning that supports a go, narrow, pause, pivot or expansion decision.

## Success is not

- downloads, impressions, page views or merchant registrations alone;
- cart additions, directions or scans labelled as sales;
- QR verified purchases labelled as payment, settlement, revenue or total trade;
- a promise that every physical product in Esenler is indexed;
- every future engine shipping on day one;
- a temporary subsidy or advertising burst presented as durable retention.

## Day-one customer promise

“Yakındaki esnafta seçili ürünleri keşfet; mağaza ve ürün bilgisinin güncellik
durumunu gör; fiziksel mağazayla etkileşime geç.” If QR is active, its explanation
adds that confirmation records an EsnaftaVar verified physical-purchase fact and
does not process payment.

## Mandatory boundaries

- No online payment, shipping, classic checkout or classic order.
- Discovery can be guest-friendly; actions needing identity may require login.
- No false stock, nearest-shop, discount, verified or regulated-product claims.
- No merchant can edit canonical product facts or another shop's listing.
- No client event can manufacture a verified purchase or review right.
- No merchant/customer personal data is exposed in dashboards beyond the approved
  operational purpose.
- No launch scope expands automatically because acquisition demand exists.

## Pilot actors

| Actor | Minimum responsibility | Cannot do |
|---|---|---|
| Customer | Use discovery truthfully; authenticate for protected actions. | Confirm a purchase unilaterally. |
| Merchant/shop operator | Attest shop/listing truth; operate exact-shop QR path. | Alter canonical facts, evidence history or another shop. |
| Pilot operator | Assist onboarding, triage cases, record evidence and escalate. | Bypass policy, server authority or hidden audit. |
| Product Owner | Decide scope, promise, budget, policy and stop/expand gates. | Delegate legal or technical evidence away by label. |
| Specialist reviewer | Advise legal/privacy/security/policy cases as engaged. | Convert an unresolved area into silent approval. |

## Readiness state

The contract is ready for Product Owner review only. It is not launch approval,
runtime implementation or Production authorization.
