# EsnaftaVar Esenler Pilot — Merchant Density Model

**State:** `PLANNING MODEL — THRESHOLDS NOT OWNER FINAL`

## Density is a service promise, not a raw count

A useful launch cell has enough independently useful, verified shops and current
listings to complete common customer discovery tasks within a credible local trip.
One hundred scattered registrations can be weaker than 15 launch-ready shops on
two adjacent commercial streets.

## Four density layers

| Layer | Measure | Planning signal | Failure signal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Geographic | Active shops per launch cell and travel corridor | Several options within the declared local area | Shop is technically in Esenler but not practically local |
| Domain | Useful sellers per promised Product L1/domain | Prefer at least `2–3` independent sellers for a marketed domain | Single-seller dependency or empty domain |
| Listing | Current useful listings per shop/domain | Working band `15–60`, with domain exceptions | Logo-only shop or bulk low-quality duplicates |
| Operational | Shops whose truth can be maintained | Verification, freshness and support within capacity | Registrations grow faster than review/update capacity |

All bands are assumptions. A domain-specific task audit decides sufficiency.

## Launch-size scenarios

| Scenario | Shops | Useful listing hypothesis | Domain strategy | Readiness interpretation |
|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| Micro proof | `12–25` | roughly `250–900` total | `3–5` ordinary domains with depth | Tests core workflow; not district coverage |
| Moderate proof | `30–60` | roughly `900–3,000` | `6–10` domains across adjacent cells | Tests repeatability and support capacity |
| Broad rollout | `75–120` | roughly `2,500–7,000` | Wider ordinary-domain coverage | Requires mature self-service, monitoring and queue ownership |

Totals are synthetic planning ranges, not commitments. Unique, discoverable,
current listings matter more than row counts.

## Coverage scorecard

For each launch cell × promised domain record:

- verified active shops;
- independently useful sellers;
- current visible listings and duplicate rate;
- representative query/task success;
- price/availability freshness distribution;
- distance/travel plausibility;
- unresolved merchant/catalog/support cases;
- customer feedback on missing choice.

Suggested status vocabulary: `EMPTY`, `THIN`, `USABLE`, `ROBUST`, `STALE`,
`PAUSED`. Exact numeric cutoffs remain an owner/operations decision after field
inventory.

## Category sequence

Start with ordinary, easily verified physical products that fit existing catalog
and listing truth. Prefer a few coherent domains over all 24 L1 labels. Policy-
signalled, high-return-risk, age-restricted, health-claim, service/booking and
ambiguous custom-product domains remain off the launch promise until separately
approved.

## Density gate

Do not acquire customers into a cell/domain that is `EMPTY`, `THIN`, `STALE` or
operationally unserviceable. Acquisition can begin only after the published
promise matches `USABLE` inventory and support capacity.
