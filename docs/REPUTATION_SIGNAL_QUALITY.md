# Merchant Reputation Signal Quality

Status: **PROPOSED — OWNER/POLICY REVIEW REQUIRED**
Wave: 18 / Workstream V

## STRONG

- Current authoritative identity/policy verification as a factual status.
- Objectively measured catalog/listing mismatch and its confirmed correction.
- Upheld security/QR abuse or policy findings with appeal outcome.
- Confirmed complaint resolution facts where governance exists.

Strong means evidence integrity, not automatically customer-visible or positive.

## MEDIUM

- Eligible review rating/count with sufficient sample and transparent display.
- Verified purchase activity as operational history, not quality.
- Availability freshness/accuracy under a measurable definition.

## WEAK

- Profile completeness, tenure, raw views/directions, response time before messaging SLA exists.
- Customer discovery/popularity without normalization.

## UNSAFE

- Advertising spend/bid/impressions.
- Reward funding/participation.
- Self-reported superlatives, merchant sector alone, raw QR scans, listing volume.
- Positive-review percentage manipulated by suppression or incentive.

## Rule

No composite score can hide underlying rating or uncertainty. Restricted negative security evidence must not be exposed as exploitable thresholds or unsupported public accusation.
