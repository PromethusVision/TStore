# Operations Appeal Model

**State:** PROPOSED FOR OWNER REVIEW

## Scope

Customers and merchants may appeal a material support, moderation, verification, policy, fraud, suspension, or account decision. An appeal is a linked reconsideration, not deletion or silent editing of the original case.

## Appeal envelope

- appeal ID, appellant type and securely verified account;
- original case/decision/policy version;
- contested reason/scope;
- new evidence or claimed process error;
- requested outcome;
- assigned reviewer and independence status;
- interim restriction decision;
- review history, outcome, reason, current policy version;
- superseding/reversal actions and communication.

## Lifecycle

`SUBMITTED → ELIGIBILITY_CHECK → IN_REVIEW → WAITING_EVIDENCE/ESCALATED → UPHELD/MODIFIED/OVERTURNED → CLOSED`.

Duplicate appeals link to the active appeal. New material evidence can reopen. A changed policy does not automatically rewrite history; reviewer records whether it applies prospectively or to the case under approved rules.

## Independence and safety

Where staffing permits, the reviewer differs from the original decision-maker for suspension, regulated denial, merge/split, verified-history correction, permanent restriction, or high-value dispute. One-person pilot records the conflict, uses structured evidence/policy checks, and receives delayed sample review when another qualified person becomes available.

Appeal does not automatically lift containment where active harm is plausible. Conversely, punishment cannot be extended merely because someone appealed. Reporter identity, fraud thresholds, and internal security notes remain protected.

## Communication

Provide decision, safe reason, evidence/remediation category, effective scope, and further escalation if available. Never promise a result, reveal detection methods, or require password/OTP.
