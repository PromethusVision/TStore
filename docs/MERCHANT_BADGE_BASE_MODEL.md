## Badge taxonomy

| Layer | Meaning | Example candidate | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| factual | verified operational fact, not quality | `Doğrulanmış mağaza` | separate verification policy |
| primary reputation | one narrow shop-experience dimension | `Güler Yüzlü Esnaf` | eligible structured responses |
| composite | multiple active primary prerequisites | candidate multi-strength badge | dependency graph |
| meta/local | composite plus regional/local standing | `Mahallenin Yıldızı` | absolute quality, locality and sample rules |

Factual verification and earned reputation are visually and semantically separate. A merchant cannot
pay for, self-assign or gain a reputation badge through rewards.

## Primary candidates

- `Güler Yüzlü Esnaf` ← friendliness dimension.
- `Yardımsever Esnaf` ← helpfulness dimension.
- `Ürün Bilgisi Güçlü` ← product-information accuracy dimension.

All names need comprehension and pressure/stigma testing. A shop with no badge is not labeled bad.

## Eligibility envelope

Each primary badge needs a versioned dimension rule, effective unique-customer sample, confidence
condition, freshness, evidence-integrity eligibility, shop-active state and absence of a relevant
fraud/policy hold. Raw average alone cannot award it.

## Explicit exclusions

- product stars/text, sales value, quantity, ad spend, reward participation and merchant self-claims;
- paid placement, sector popularity and organization size;
- platform/operator discretionary score edits;
- a badge inferred from sentiment analysis of product free text.
