# EsnaftaVar Esenler Pilot — Merchant Retention and Engagement

**State:** `MEASUREMENT/INTERVENTION OPTIONS — NO PROMISE`

## Retention means operational value

A retained merchant remains verified, keeps a useful truthful catalog, can operate
the chosen customer/QR path and chooses to continue after the relevant commercial
terms are clear. Login frequency alone is not retention.

## Candidate signals

- active shop/listing state and freshness completion;
- useful listings kept current;
- successful customer discovery/directions and QR coverage, correctly labelled;
- merchant support burden and unresolved friction;
- merchant-reported value and willingness to continue;
- voluntary continuation at offer/milestone transition;
- pause/exit reasons.

## Lean engagement cadence

1. onboarding follow-up after first maintenance task;
2. first-week truth and QR review;
3. short periodic catalog-health reminder;
4. issue-specific support, not generic spam;
5. milestone interview before changing commercial terms;
6. clear pause/exit and correction path.

## Churn-risk actions

| Signal | Safe response | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Stale catalog | Ask for bounded refresh or pause affected listings | Pretend availability |
| No customer interaction | Check density/search truth before promising promotion | Call the merchant unsuccessful from views alone |
| QR friction | Observe training/device flow and reconcile outcomes | Award manual purchase evidence |
| High support time | Simplify scope/process or cap growth | Hide operator cost |
| Commercial uncertainty | Present explicit options and end state | Silent conversion from free to paid |

Ads, rewards or reputation should not be activated merely to repair weak core
merchant retention.
