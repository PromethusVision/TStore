# False-Positive Safeguards

**State:** PROPOSED FOR OWNER REVIEW

## Before action

- distinguish report/signal from substantiated fact;
- show evidence provenance, conflicts, freshness, and confidence;
- require exact subject/version and policy version;
- consider technical outage, account takeover, shared device/shop, catalog ambiguity, and legitimate edge case;
- preview blast radius and choose narrow reversible containment;
- second review for broad/permanent/high-value actions;
- do not let count or opaque score decide alone.

## During action

Use temporary hold where investigation is incomplete; communicate non-accusatory safe reason; preserve business/customer access outside affected scope; set review target; offer evidence/appeal; audit all assumptions.

## After action

Sample decisions, measure appeal/overturn/reopen/restoration and harm duration, analyze cohorts without discriminatory profiling, correct the source event/policy/control, restore through superseding action, communicate material correction, and preserve operator-error evidence.

## Automation

Automated classifier/rules may route, deduplicate, or recommend. High-impact decisions require explainability and human/governed approval unless an owner-final deterministic safety rule exists. Detector uncertainty fails closed for approval but punitive action remains proportionate.

## Metrics

False-positive estimate by case/action/policy version, time-to-restore, appeal overturn, reviewer disagreement, recurrence after restoration, and affected dependent objects. Never optimize by discouraging appeals.
