# Türkiye Sponsored Advertising and Commercial Practice Audit

**State:** PROPOSED FAIL-CLOSED FOUNDATION — NOT LEGAL ADVICE  
**Research cut-off:** 2026-08-28

Catalog eligibility, merchant eligibility and advertising eligibility are three
independent gates. A lawful ordinary listing can still have an unlawful claim,
audience, ranking treatment or incentive.

## Disclosure contract

1. Every paid placement shows the exact Turkish word **`Sponsorlu`** at first render.
2. Text remains visible in list/grid/compact/accessibility/loading/image-error states.
3. It sits close to the paid card and cannot be moved to a details screen, tooltip
   or only-color/icon signal.
4. Advertiser/shop identity is visible; payment never creates `Önerilen`, `En İyi`,
   `En Yakın`, `Doğrulandı`, `Fırsat` or organic-ranking meaning.
5. A reachable **“Neden Sponsorlu?”** explanation states the main paid/contextual/
   local criteria and available controls without exposing anti-abuse rules.
6. Served creative/revision/label/policy and material decision inputs are preserved
   for complaint evidence under an approved retention rule.
7. A surface variant unable to render the disclosure is ineligible to serve.

The text choice is a strong V1 product recommendation, not legal finalization or
final UI approval.

## Targeting and personalization

| Practice | V1 recommendation | Reason/gate |
|---|---|---|
| Current surface/category/product context | allow candidate | explainable and low-data |
| Current query after sensitive-pattern guard | allow candidate | no long-lived profile |
| Bounded current local context | allow candidate after privacy review | no precise history/merchant visitor map |
| General behavioral profile | defer | not necessary for Esenler pilot |
| Third-party ad ID/data-broker audience | exclude | high privacy/role/transfer burden |
| Uploaded customer/contact list | exclude | consent/source/role risk |
| Cross-app retargeting | exclude | unnecessary pilot surveillance |
| Health/regulated purchase inference | exclude | sensitive and claim risk |
| Profiled child targeting | fail closed | current advertising direction and child risk |

Organic discovery remains available when optional ad processing is declined. Ad
measurement and product analytics are separate purposes; security evidence cannot
be reused for targeting.

## Claim audit

| Claim family | Required evidence | Default when missing/conflicting |
|---|---|---|
| Price | same listing currency/unit/effective time | do not serve price claim |
| Discount/comparison price | current legal comparison method and historical source | block `indirim`/strikethrough |
| Availability/urgency | fresh merchant assertion and exact meaning | no “son ürün”, countdown or artificial scarcity |
| Cheapest/best/top | defined comparator, complete evidence and current period | prohibit candidate |
| Nearest/local | approved location input and measurable rule | no absolute claim from sponsorship |
| Authorized/verified | exact evidence scope and badge definition | do not claim |
| Health/safety/performance | current authorized product/claim evidence | regulatory review or block |
| Environmental/ethical | substantiation and precise scope | block vague green/ethical claim |
| Customer rating | current eligible-review projection | no selective/stale/paid manipulation |

## Unfair-practice and dark-pattern guard

No preselected marketing consent, disguised ad, false countdown, difficult opt-out,
forced optional tracking, hidden material term, bait price, fabricated scarcity,
review suppression, subscription trap or confusing button hierarchy. A/B testing
does not excuse a prohibited pattern.

## Governance

Campaign creation does not publish. Server-authoritative serving rechecks merchant,
listing, product, claim, policy, audience, budget and disclosure. Policy changes stop
serving without erasing history. Appeals preserve original decision and evidence;
an operator cannot grant a one-off exception outside an approved ruleset.
