# EsnaftaVar Customer Analytics Visibility Review

**State:** `CONTRARIAN REVIEW — RECOMMEND NO CUSTOMER ANALYTICS DASHBOARD`

The customer app's job is nearby discovery and physical-shop interaction. A
personal “analytics dashboard” adds tracking pressure, complexity and weak value
without a clear customer decision it supports.

Useful customer-facing facts should live in their product contexts instead:
verified-purchase history, review status, wishlist/cart state, notification state
and privacy/account controls. They are not an analytics dashboard.

Do not expose behavioral scores, time spent, inferred interests, location history,
ad profile, gamification comparison or “loyalty” unless a future owner-approved
feature has clear benefit and privacy/legal basis.

Recommendation is no V1 customer analytics dashboard. Product Owner has not
finalized this decision.

`CUSTOMER_ANALYTICS_DASHBOARD_RECOMMENDATION: NO`
