# Reward Economics Model

Status: **PROPOSED — FINANCIAL/LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED; NO PROMISE**
Wave: 18 / Workstream F

## Funding options

| Funder | Strength | Risk |
|---|---|---|
| Merchant | Direct loyalty intent and bounded shop economics | Merchant comprehension, margin, closure/dispute |
| Platform | Uniform experience and launch control | Central liability, budget and fairness |
| Shared | Cost/risk split | Clearing and explanation complexity |
| Marketing campaign budget | Bounded experiment | Campaign expiry and earned-right continuity |

## Economic facts that must be explicit

- Who owes the benefit and under which entity/program.
- When liability is recognized, reserved, redeemed, expired or reversed.
- Maximum exposure/caps and what happens when funding ends.
- Whether benefit has monetary equivalence, tax/accounting treatment or transferability.
- Fraud loss ownership and dispute/appeal route.
- Merchant/customer terms version effective at earning time.

## Risks

Margin erosion, reward farming, merchant insolvency/closure, misleading balance, retroactive devaluation, cross-merchant subsidy, unclaimed liability, regulated-product incentive and support overhead.

## Recommendation

Do not promise platform-wide points/credits. If Product Owner later approves a test, use a capped, merchant-specific, non-transferable benefit with locked terms and shadow-mode economics first. This is not financial/accounting/legal advice.
