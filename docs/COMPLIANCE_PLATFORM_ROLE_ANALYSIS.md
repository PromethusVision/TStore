# EsnaftaVar Platform Role Analysis

**State:** ROLE HYPOTHESES — LAWYER REVIEW REQUIRED  
**Business-model snapshot:** EsnaftaVar currently provides local discovery,
merchant/shop listings, prices/availability asserted by merchants, QR purchase
verification and eligible customer reviews. It does not presently take payment,
sell the goods, arrange shipment or issue a receipt.

## What the current facts support

- A merchant remains the source of the product, availability and price assertion.
- A QR verification is evidence that an in-person event was confirmed; it is not a
  payment authorization, fiscal receipt, invoice or audited revenue statement.
- EsnaftaVar still makes independent decisions about accounts, ranking, review
  publication, moderation, analytics, security and future ads. “No payment” does
  not mean “no platform obligations.”
- If later functionality lets customers order, contract, pay or arrange delivery,
  the statutory analysis must be repeated before launch.

## Change triggers

Mandatory re-review is triggered by in-app ordering, payment collection, delivery,
subscription, paid merchant account, cross-merchant reward value, consumer dispute
handling on the underlying sale, stored funds, external ad networks, children-focused
services, or marketplace fulfilment.

## Fail-closed wording rules

- Do not call EsnaftaVar the seller, payment provider, delivery provider or tax
  intermediary unless the model actually changes and is reviewed.
- Do not claim “officially approved shop,” “guaranteed price,” “audited sale,” or
  “legal product” from a category, badge or QR event.
- Do not use “we are only a directory” as a universal liability waiver.
