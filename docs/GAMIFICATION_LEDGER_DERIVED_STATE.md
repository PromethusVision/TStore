# Immutable Ledger vs Derived State

Status: **PROPOSED — NO DB DESIGN**
Wave: 18 / Workstream AP

## Immutable/append-only candidates

- Verified purchase and review source-event references.
- Reward EARN/ADJUST/REVERSE/REDEEM/EXPIRE entries.
- Badge awarded/revoked/retired/superseded decisions and rule version.
- Reputation evidence observations, corrections and policy decisions.
- Dispute/appeal outcomes and restricted actor/provenance.

## Derived/recalculable

- Reward balance and bar percentage.
- Active badge list and progress.
- Reputation badge eligibility/current factual status.
- Time-windowed analytics/health metrics.

## Snapshot requirements

Historical display meaning, unit/value, product/shop references and applicable terms must remain explainable even after names, taxonomy, catalog or rules change.

## Boundary

Recalculation cannot invent missing authoritative evidence, duplicate rewards, erase customer ratings or make ad spend a trust signal. Projection repair changes derived state; source history remains.
