# Terms, Notices, Support and Mobile Disclosure Dependencies

**State:** REQUIREMENTS INVENTORY — NO LEGAL TEXT DRAFTED OR APPROVED

## Customer-facing document set

| Surface | Minimum purpose | Professional owner |
|---|---|---|
| Customer terms of use | platform service, account rules, content/review, merchant relationship, QR boundaries, suspension/appeal, change process | `LAWYER` + `PRODUCT_OWNER` |
| Activity-layered privacy notice | controller/contact, data/purposes/grounds/recipients/method/rights/transfers | `KVKK/PRIVACY_SPECIALIST` |
| Optional preference/consent controls | analytics/ads/commercial messages where applicable | privacy specialist + lawyer |
| Community/review rules | eligibility, publication, moderation, response/report, correction/appeal | lawyer + owner |
| Support/contact page | reachable channels, safe ownership verification, expected scope, privacy requests | owner + operations + privacy |
| QR/verified-purchase explanation | what is verified and what is not | lawyer + owner |
| Sponsored explanation | `Sponsorlu`, material criteria, controls/report | lawyer + owner + privacy |
| Account deletion/data request route | closure effects, request route and identity safety | privacy specialist + lawyer |

## Merchant-facing document set

- merchant/platform terms and shop/listing accuracy duties;
- exact verification and badge meaning;
- sector/capability/product/claim evidence obligations;
- price/availability freshness and correction;
- QR/staff/security rules;
- review response/report boundaries;
- advertising/reward terms only if those modules launch;
- suspension, appeal, document retention and policy-change process;
- privacy roles for merchant-provided staff/customer data.

No document may say merchant verification legalizes every product, that QR is payment
or tax proof, that prices are guaranteed, or that EsnaftaVar endorses sponsored goods.

## Support minimum

Pilot needs at least one monitored customer route and one merchant route, plus a
privacy/data-request channel and a security/abuse escalation. Operators never request
passwords, OTPs, confirmation/recovery links, service keys or unrelated identity
documents. Contact details must be real and reachable before publication; this task
does not invent them.

## Store/mobile disclosures

Apple requires app/third-party privacy practices and a privacy policy URL; Google
Play requires accurate Data Safety declarations, privacy policy and relevant deletion
representation. Store forms must match the exact release artifact, SDKs and Production
flows. Store labels do not replace KVKK notice/ground/rights work, and a policy URL
does not prove operational deletion.

## Version/change controls

Documents carry version/effective date, scope, responsible entity and change summary.
Material changes are surfaced before or when they take effect under approved rules;
optional consent is reacquired only where required. Old versions remain available to
interpret historical decisions without retaining unnecessary PII.
