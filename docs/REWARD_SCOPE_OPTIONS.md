# Reward Scope Options

Status: **PROPOSED — MAJOR OWNER DECISION**
Wave: 18 / Workstream E

| Scope | Earn/redeem boundary | Benefit | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| Merchant loyalty | Same merchant organization or defined shop program | Funding and promise understandable | Branch/closure portability |
| Platform loyalty | Across EsnaftaVar merchants | Simple customer narrative | Platform liability and clearing |
| Category loyalty | Across eligible product/category identities | Discovery potential | Taxonomy change and policy incentives |
| Mixed | Several concurrent scopes | Flexible | Confusing balances and disputes |

## Recommendation

Merchant-specific program is the smallest defensible economic scope, but should remain post-pilot until funding, closure and dispute rules exist. Platform-wide and category-wide value transfer are separate P0 decisions, not automatic future upgrades.

## Boundary rules

- Merchant identity and shop/branch scope are explicit; renames do not reset progress.
- Cross-merchant redemption requires funding/clearing responsibility before any earn event.
- Category scope uses stable IDs/versioned lineage, never mutable names/paths.
- Policy-sensitive domains can be ineligible even if the merchant participates.
- No reward scope changes customer review rights or merchant reputation.
