# Reward Expiry Options

Status: **PROPOSED — OWNER/POLICY REVIEW REQUIRED**
Wave: 18 / Workstream H

| Model | Trust benefit | Economic benefit | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| No expiry | Maximum customer clarity | Indefinite obligation | Merchant closure/long liability |
| Fixed platform expiry | Consistent explanation | Bounded liability | Arbitrary loss if too short |
| Merchant-defined expiry | Fits merchant economics | Local flexibility | Inconsistent/unfair programs |
| Campaign-defined expiry | Bounded promotion | Controlled budget | Progress may outlive campaign |

## Trust rules

- Expiry terms are shown before earning and at all balance/progress surfaces.
- No hidden countdown, artificial urgency or retroactive shortening.
- Notifications are helpful and frequency-capped; non-delivery does not change legal/product outcome.
- Expiry is an immutable ledger event with terms version, not deletion.
- Suspended/closed merchant, account deletion, appeal and outage behavior are separate.

## Recommendation

No economic reward should launch until expiry/liability review. Trust preference is no expiry for already earned value; if unsustainable, use a long, platform-governed fixed term established before earning—not arbitrary merchant edits.
