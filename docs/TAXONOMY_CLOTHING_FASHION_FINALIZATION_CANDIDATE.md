The table above is the complete candidate leaf registry. Every garment receives one primary leaf. Gender, age group, size, color, material, fit, season and style remain facets. A coordinated set is one sellable product only when offered as one package; visual compatibility alone does not create a set category.

## 7. Inclusion/exclusion examples

**Included:** shirts, trousers, dresses, coats, underwear, pajamas, performance garments, swimwear and ordinary uniforms.

**Excluded or routed elsewhere:** shoes, bags, jewelry, eyewear, wearable electronics, protective helmets/respirators, garment rental/alteration services and fabric sold by length.

## 8. High-impact L3 choices

- Technical sport garments are separated by product construction and use intent, not by styling or athlete endorsement.
- `Spor Sütyeni` is performance-first; ordinary lingerie remains `İç Giyim`.
- `Teknik İç Katman` is performance/base-layer clothing; ordinary thermal underwear remains `İç Giyim`.
- Certified PPE cannot be made ordinary clothing by naming it “iş giyimi”.
- `Ferace & Abaya` remains a product-type leaf, but owner terminology review is retained.

## 9. Facet and synonym hints
