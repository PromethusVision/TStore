## 5. Primary assignment and boundaries

- A toy-class drone whose primary purpose is play stays here; an imaging/camera drone routes to Elektronik → Fotoğraf & Kamera. Flight capability alone is not sufficient.
- A tuneable, performance-capable instrument routes to Müzik & Enstrüman; a role-play sound toy stays here. Target age or price alone does not decide.
- General paint, paper and craft supplies remain Kırtasiye & Ofis; a complete directed kit stays here.
- Real sports equipment routes to Spor & Outdoor; play-scale and toy-safety products stay here.
- Consoles, controllers and video-game hardware remain Elektronik. Digital codes, subscriptions and services do not enter Product Taxonomy.
- Firearms, ammunition, explosives and weapon-like high-risk recreation have no normal toy leaf. Airsoft/paintball and realistic projectiles remain fail-closed professional review/exclusion candidates.
