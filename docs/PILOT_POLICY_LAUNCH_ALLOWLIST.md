# EsnaftaVar Esenler Pilot — Policy Launch Allowlist Framework

**State:** `FRAMEWORK ONLY — NO DOMAIN AUTHORIZED`

## Default

Unknown or policy-signalled merchant/product activity is excluded until the
Product Owner and responsible legal/policy/operations reviewers approve scope,
evidence, customer wording, reviewer and incident route. Taxonomy placement is not
permission.

## Ordinary-domain first criteria

An initial domain candidate should have:

- ordinary physical retail purpose and clear product identity;
- low age/health/safety/financial/controlled-goods risk;
- no medical or performance claim needing specialist evidence;
- straightforward price/availability truth;
- usable canonical catalog coverage and limited candidate ambiguity;
- manageable returns/dispute expectation despite no online sale;
- several suitable merchants in the selected launch cell.

## Default excluded review groups

Pending current professional review, keep off the launch allowlist: alcohol,
tobacco/nicotine, medicines, supplements/health claims, medical devices and health
services, weapons/hunting/pyrotechnics, regulated chemicals, live animals,
age-restricted products, financial/credit services, and booking/service-completion
claims without a completion contract. This list is conservative and not legal
advice.

## Allowlist row contract

`scope_id, taxonomy/sector identity and version, allowed/excluded examples,
required evidence, reviewer role, effective dates, customer wording, monitoring,
incident/pause path, review date`.

## Release rule

Absence from the approved allowlist means no publication or marketing promise. A
merchant's broader real-world inventory does not expand the platform scope.

Commercial messaging must be honest and accurate; personal-data processing must
have a specified, legitimate, limited purpose and appropriate safeguards. Current
professional review remains required before launch. Reference points:

- [T.C. Ticaret Bakanlığı — Ticari Reklamlar](https://ticaret.gov.tr/tuketici/ticari-reklamlar)
- [KVKK — General Principles in Processing of Personal Data](https://www.kvkk.gov.tr/Icerik/6606/General-Principles-in-Processing-of-Personal-Data)
