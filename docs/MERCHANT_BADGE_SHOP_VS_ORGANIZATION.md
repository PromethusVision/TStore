# Shop versus Merchant-Organization Reputation

**State:** SHOP-FIRST RECOMMENDATION — ORGANIZATION ROLL-UP DEFERRED

## Primary scope

The exact shop/branch confirms the QR and delivers the local experience. Structured evaluation, effective
contribution, primary badge and local meta-badge therefore attach to **shop** first.

## Multi-branch rules

- Each branch accumulates its own evidence and badges.
- One branch's score/badge cannot fan out to siblings.
- Organization pages may later show a clearly labeled statistical roll-up, never a copied badge.
- Staff movement does not transfer historical shop evidence.
- Cross-branch customer contributions are independently capped per shop/window.

## Organization roll-up options

| Option | Benefit | Risk |
|---|---|---|
| none | maximum clarity | no chain overview |
| response-weighted | reflects volume | large branch dominates |
| equal-branch | prevents volume dominance | tiny branch over-weighting |
| hierarchical model | models uncertainty | complexity and opaque priors |

No option is selected. A future roll-up must preserve branch distributions and `insufficient history`.

## Lifecycle

Rename preserves shop identity. Relocation creates region-effective periods. Shop merge/split requires
lineage mapping, not averaging. Closure retires active badges while keeping history. Ownership transfer
requires owner policy before badge continuity; historical review text remains attached to its event lineage.
