# Privacy Policy Release Dependency

**State:** RELEASE DEPENDENCY — NOT LEGAL ADVICE

Before commercial release, customer-facing privacy information must accurately cover actual data collection/use/sharing/retention for account/profile, location, QR/purchase/review, chat/media, notifications, analytics/crash reporting, support/operations and account requests.

## QA dependency checks

- published URL is reachable in app and store surfaces;
- text matches enabled features/providers and Production data flows;
- permission prompts and store disclosures are consistent;
- version/effective date and contact route are visible;
- changes are reviewed when telemetry, ads/rewards, Merchant App or processors change.

QA verifies consistency and availability, not legal sufficiency. No policy text or retention rule is finalized here.

OWNER_DECISION_REQUIRED: obtain qualified privacy/legal review and approve the published policy/version.
