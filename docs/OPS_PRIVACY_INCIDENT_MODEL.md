# Privacy Incident Model

**State:** PROPOSED — NOT LEGAL ADVICE OR NOTIFICATION DECISION

## Potential incidents

Unauthorized access/disclosure, wrong-recipient support response, exposed token/identity document/location/chat/review evidence, cross-account/merchant data access, insecure export, excessive operator browsing, lost operator device, vendor/subprocessor incident, retention failure, or inability to delete/restrict data as approved.

## Response

1. Create protected incident record and preserve minimal evidence.
2. Contain access/disclosure; revoke credentials/sessions and stop unsafe processing.
3. Identify data categories, subjects, systems, recipients, time, cause, protection state, and continuing risk.
4. Engage designated privacy/security/legal decision owner immediately.
5. Determine notification/authority obligations from current law and facts.
6. Notify only through approved process and preserve record.
7. Recover, verify deletion/access corrections where appropriate, monitor misuse, and complete postmortem.

## Data minimization

Incident case access is highly restricted. Do not duplicate exposed data to explain exposure. Use counts/categories and protected references. Reporter/affected-person communication excludes attack details that create further risk.

## Research anchor

The [KVKK personal-data breach notification decision](https://www.kvkk.gov.tr/Icerik/5362/Veri-Ihlali-Bildirimi) interprets notification to the Board as without delay and no later than 72 hours after awareness, calls for prompt affected-person communication, records of effects/measures, and a reviewed response plan. Responsible legal/privacy owners must assess applicability; operators do not make the legal conclusion alone.
