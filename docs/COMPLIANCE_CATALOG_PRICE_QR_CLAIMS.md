# Catalog Provenance, Price, Availability and QR Claim Boundaries

**State:** PRODUCT/LEGAL CLAIM FOUNDATION — NO RUNTIME

## Catalog provenance

Canonical identity proves which product the system means; it does not prove lawful
sale, authenticity, conformity, health benefit or merchant authorization. Every
material assertion needs source, actor, observed/effective time, evidence class,
confidence/status and policy version. Merchant self-assertion is labeled as such.

Product merge/split/reclassification preserves predecessor/successor lineage,
listings, reviews, QR evidence, policy decisions and aliases. A split never silently
assigns ambiguous historical evidence to one child. Policy status changes current
eligibility without rewriting what happened.

## Price and availability

| Claim | Safe minimum |
|---|---|
| Current price | currency, amount, unit/basis, source merchant, observed/effective time |
| Availability | defined state, source and freshness; stale/unknown visibly distinct |
| Discount | legally valid comparison basis and evidence; no arbitrary strikethrough |
| Cheapest | complete defined comparator and current evidence; otherwise prohibited |
| Nearest | approved current location and measurable rule; sponsorship cannot create it |
| In stock | current merchant assertion, not guaranteed reservation |

Price is local-discovery information, not payment settlement, checkout promise,
invoice, receipt or tax document. Ads use the same current listing truth as organic
results. Merchant correction appends provenance; it does not rewrite historical QR
item/price snapshot.

## QR verified purchase

QR confirmation can establish that authorized customer and merchant sessions
confirmed a single-use transaction context and item snapshot. It does **not** by
itself establish:

- payment method or successful fund transfer;
- fiscal receipt/invoice correctness;
- audited merchant revenue, tax or accounting treatment;
- product legality/authenticity/conformity;
- customer satisfaction or endorsement;
- eligibility for ads or economic rewards.

The customer and merchant see the bounded meaning before confirmation. Replay,
wrong-shop, customer-only confirmation, expiry and concurrent-confirmation defenses
are security requirements; they do not expand the legal claim.

## Review evidence

An eligible review may state that it is based on EsnaftaVar's defined QR verification.
The badge explanation and policy page disclose the method and limitations. A later
`DISPUTED`, `VOIDED`, `INVALIDATED` or `SUPERSEDED` correction preserves original
evidence and deterministically updates review eligibility/rating under an owner-final
rule; no silent deletion or fabricated replacement.
