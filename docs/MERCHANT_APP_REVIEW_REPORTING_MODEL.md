# Merchant App Review Reporting Model

Status: **PROPOSED — POLICY OWNER REVIEW REQUIRED**
Wave: 17 / WP47

## Principle

Reporting requests governed platform review; it is not merchant deletion, suppression or automatic ranking control.

## Flow

1. Merchant selects an eligible reason class and optional minimized explanation/evidence.
2. Server verifies shop scope and prevents duplicate open reports.
3. Review remains under canonical visibility policy while pending.
4. Authorized moderation returns upheld/rejected/needs-info outcome.
5. Merchant receives safe reason and appeal path; audit remains.

## Candidate reason classes

Spam, harassment, unrelated content, prohibited personal data, impersonation, legal/policy concern. “Negative rating” is not a valid reason.

## Safeguards

- Rate limits and abuse detection for mass reporting.
- No access to customer private identity.
- Reporting does not alter verified purchase evidence.
- Reviewer conflict-of-interest and escalation ownership require operations definition.

Open decisions: reason taxonomy, temporary visibility during severe reports, appeal policy and SLA.
