# Merchant App Customer Trust Boundaries

Status: **PROPOSED — NON-NEGOTIABLE CONTRACTS**
Wave: 17 / WP115

Merchant actions must not manipulate:

- Customer reviews, ratings or review eligibility.
- Verified transaction/item evidence.
- Canonical product identity through local listing edits.
- Organic ranking through payment or merchant flags.
- Badge/reputation result or future reward ledger.
- Availability wording so unknown appears guaranteed in stock.
- Analytics labels so views/directions appear as sales/visits.

## Allowed merchant influence

Accurate own-shop public profile/location/status, eligible listing price/availability, allowed local media, physical QR confirmation at authorized shop, governed review report and future clearly disclosed ad campaign request.

## Evidence separation

Payment/ads, reputation, rewards, merchant sector and customer review are separate from QR verified evidence. A successful QR is not payment settlement; a paid campaign cannot create verification or badge.

## Transparency

Customer-facing changes show actual shop/listing identity and freshness. Sponsored placement must display persistent `Sponsorlu` and preserve organic fallback. Errors never fabricate success or hide stale truth.
