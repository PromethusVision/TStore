# EsnaftaVar Türkiye Compliance Master Blueprint

**State:** READY FOR PROFESSIONAL AND PRODUCT-OWNER REVIEW
**Research cut-off:** 2026-08-28
**Legal status:** foundation only; not legal, tax or accounting advice.

## Product posture

EsnaftaVar currently presents local merchant/product discovery, merchant-sourced listings and
price/availability signals, QR-based physical-purchase verification and eligible customer reviews.
It does not currently present itself as seller, payment recipient, carrier, fiscal receipt issuer or
audited revenue system. Counsel must determine the legal roles of the exact released capabilities.

## Separation rules

```text
taxonomy eligibility
  != catalog eligibility
  != merchant authorization
  != listing eligibility
  != advertising eligibility
  != reward eligibility
```

Merchant sector is not regulatory authorization. “Verified purchase” is bounded evidence, not a
payment, invoice or authenticity guarantee. Ads and rewards cannot improve review/reputation truth.

## V1 control plane

1. Freeze exact pilot features, data flows, vendors and regulated exclusions.
2. Route the 32 professional questions and 12 owner root decisions without merging their authority.
3. Keep exact location transient, analytics aggregate/necessary, optional profiling off.
4. Publish reviewed notices, terms dependencies, claim explanations and a reachable support route.
5. Verify ordinary merchants proportionately; gate regulated capabilities and products separately.
6. Keep sponsored ads and economic rewards off until their dedicated gates close.
7. Preserve review parity, QR evidence history, decision reasons and appeal.
8. Enforce least privilege, security/breach procedure and auditable data-rights/deletion workflows.
9. Reconcile store declarations with the exact shipping artifact.
10. Revalidate sources and professional answers before commercial launch or material expansion.

## Pilot conclusion

A narrow ordinary-product Esenler pilot can avoid enterprise bureaucracy by disabling unresolved
ads, economic rewards, broad chat/media and regulated inventory. It still requires truthful role and
claim surfaces, privacy/rights/security controls, merchant baseline, item-level exclusion, support
and exact-release declarations.
