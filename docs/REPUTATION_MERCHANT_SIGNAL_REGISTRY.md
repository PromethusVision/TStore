# Merchant Reputation Signal Registry

Status: **PROPOSED — SIGNALS ARE NOT FINAL WEIGHTS**
Wave: 18 / Workstream U

| ID | Signal | Candidate scope | Quality hypothesis | Caveat |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| RS-01 | Verified merchant identity/policy state | Organization/shop | STRONG factual | Verification ≠ quality |
| RS-02 | Eligible verified-purchase volume | Shop/product/time | MEDIUM | Volume favors incumbents/scale |
| RS-03 | Eligible customer rating | Shop/product/time | MEDIUM | Sample size and rating remains separately visible |
| RS-04 | Eligible review count | Shop/product/time | MEDIUM | Count ≠ quality; cold start |
| RS-05 | Listing identity/price accuracy | Listing/shop | STRONG when objectively measured | Needs correction/measurement contract |
| RS-06 | Availability accuracy/freshness | Listing/shop | MEDIUM | Unknown vs false assertion distinction |
| RS-07 | Upheld QR abuse/security history | Merchant/shop/staff | STRONG restricted negative | Appeal and privacy required |
| RS-08 | Shop profile completeness | Shop | WEAK | Easy to game; completeness ≠ quality |
| RS-09 | Customer/merchant response behavior | Shop | WEAK/TBD | Messaging/service contract absent |
| RS-10 | Upheld complaint/policy outcomes | Merchant/shop | STRONG restricted | Complaint alone is not proof |
| RS-11 | Merchant tenure | Merchant/shop | WEAK | Must not block newcomers |
| RS-12 | Ad spend/campaign history | Ad account | UNSAFE | Paid influence prohibited |
| RS-13 | Reward participation/funding | Program | UNSAFE | Economic participation ≠ trust |
| RS-14 | Merchant sector | Merchant taxonomy | UNSAFE as quality | Classification only |

Signal definitions, windows, thresholds, visibility and weighting remain owner/policy decisions.
