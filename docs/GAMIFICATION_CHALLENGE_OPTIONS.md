# Gamification Challenge Options

Status: **PROPOSED — OWNER REVIEW REQUIRED**
Wave: 18 / Workstream R

| Challenge type | Example | Risk | Posture |
|---|---|---|---|
| Non-spend discovery | Explore educational category/shop content | Weak evidence, notification spam | Possible private pilot |
| Local diversity | Discover different local categories | Location/privacy and superficial taps | Research |
| Verified purchases across merchants | Support several merchants | Unnecessary-spending incentive | Defer/high caution |
| Contribution | Submit eligible reviews/corrections | Review spam/quality gaming | Strict caps and sentiment neutrality |
| Merchant operational | Keep catalog/profile accurate | Merchant reputation gaming | Separate Merchant App domain |

## Safe challenge rules

- Participation optional; no loss/penalty for non-participation.
- No countdown/artificial scarcity designed to pressure purchase.
- Do not require alcohol, medical, supplements or other policy-sensitive purchases.
- Caps, dedup, fraud holds and correction behavior are explicit.
- Positive rating, ad engagement and raw QR scanning are never completion criteria.

## Recommendation

Challenges do not belong in the first commercial pilot. If later tested, start with non-spend educational/discovery behavior and measure trust—not engagement volume.
