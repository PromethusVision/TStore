# Gamification Success Metrics

Status: **PROPOSED — OWNER REVIEW REQUIRED**
Wave: 18 / Workstream AW

| Goal | Candidate metric | Guardrail |
|---|---|---|
| Legitimate local value | Eligible verified local purchases under defined baseline | Not forced spend or ad attribution claim |
| Merchant diversity | Privacy-safe multi-merchant discovery/support | Correct for access/new-merchant fairness |
| Contribution quality | Eligible unique reviews and moderation outcomes | No positive-rating incentive |
| Merchant operations | Listing accuracy/freshness improvement | Not raw edit volume |
| Reward usefulness | Earn-to-redeem with dispute/expiry context | Redemption rate alone can pressure spending |
| Trust | Understanding, complaint, opt-out and dark-pattern findings | Qualitative research required |
| Safety | Fraud hold/reversal/false-positive/appeal outcomes | No public risk score |

## Anti-metrics

Daily active streak, notification-open rate, badge count, ad clicks or points issued are not standalone product success. Merchant retention is interpreted alongside burden/cost and cannot justify unfair lock-in.
