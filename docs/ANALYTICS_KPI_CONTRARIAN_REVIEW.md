# EsnaftaVar KPI Contrarian Review

**State:** `RECOMMENDATION — OWNER NOT FINAL`

Avoid vanity metrics without business context:

- downloads without first usable discovery or active use;
- raw page/product views without invalid traffic and unique/session definition;
- registered accounts without activation/retention purpose;
- merchant signups without verified/active/catalog-ready state;
- QR scans without server-authoritative verified outcomes;
- directions requests labelled visits or conversions;
- gross ad impressions without qualification/validity;
- reward points/badges without ledger/value/behavior policy.

Prefer a balanced pilot set: active catalog-ready merchants, discovery coverage,
directions intent, authoritative verified purchases, QR/release health, listing
freshness and review integrity. Every KPI states what it cannot prove.

One number should not combine growth, health, trust and monetization. Targets need
baseline and Product Owner approval.
