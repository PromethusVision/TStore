# EsnaftaVar Commercialization Critical Path

**State:** RECOMMENDED — OWNER/RELEASE GATES NOT SELECTED

## Critical path

| Order | Gate | Evidence required | Blocks |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | ROOT-01 platform/release scope | named platform, signed exact artifact, smoke contract | release claim |
| 2 | Customer closeout | startup, discovery, location, auth, account lifecycle | customer pilot |
| 3 | Catalog truth | canonical/listing identity, active visibility, no demo ambiguity | search/seller accuracy |
| 4 | Merchant authority minimum | exact shop membership/capability and revocation | QR verification |
| 5 | QR transaction safety | expiry, wrong shop, replay and concurrency one-winner | verified purchase |
| 6 | Review invariants | immutable evidence and one active review/customer/product | trustworthy review |
| 7 | Policy allowlist | ordinary pilot domains and fail-closed exclusions | merchant/product activation |
| 8 | Lean operations | incident owner, evidence, reversal/correction runbook | safe live operation |
| 9 | Minimum observability | health, QR failure, release and abuse signals | controlled rollout |
| 10 | Physical acceptance | exact signed artifact on target device and backend | GO decision |

## Parallel but not critical-path work

Taxonomy runtime migration, broader facets, full Merchant App UX and iOS readiness
may proceed if they do not destabilize the ten gates. Ads, Reward, Gamification and
advanced Reputation must not consume critical-path capacity.

## Stop conditions

- Production/development identity uncertainty.
- Unresolved role escalation, cross-shop mutation or service-role exposure.
- QR permits replay, wrong-shop confirmation or duplicate verified purchases.
- Catalog identity makes review/product history ambiguous.
- Exact release artifact or physical acceptance is unavailable.

## Commercial decision boundary

Passing the technical path demonstrates a controlled-pilot candidate. It does not
approve legal policy, store distribution, merchant economics, advertising billing or
reward liability.
