# Reward Merchant Control Options

Status: **PROPOSED — MAJOR OWNER DECISION**
Wave: 18 / Workstream AJ

| Control model | Merchant can configure | Risk |
|---|---|---|
| No merchant controls | Nothing; platform programs only | Low flexibility, platform liability |
| Template controls | Choose approved benefit, threshold/range, dates within guards | Recommended hypothesis; still funding/support work |
| Full custom rules | Formula, products, expiry, benefit | Unfair changes, policy and explanation complexity |

## Non-negotiable boundaries

- Merchant cannot edit customer ledger, source purchase, fraud decision, review right, rating or reputation.
- Published terms are versioned; already earned progress/value cannot be retroactively reduced.
- Lowering/ending funding requires clear future-effective behavior and closure plan.
- Policy-sensitive products and cross-merchant transfer cannot be enabled by merchant toggle.
- Staff permission is narrower than owner/program administrator and server-authoritative.

## Recommendation

If merchant-funded reward is later approved, use a small platform-governed template catalog. Full formula builder is deferred.
