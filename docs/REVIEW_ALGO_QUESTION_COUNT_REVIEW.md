# Shop Question Count Review

**State:** EXPERIMENT OPTIONS — NO COUNT FINALIZED

## Options

| Form | Questions | Benefit | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| One | overall only | fastest, easiest pilot | hides why experience was good/bad; cannot support dimension badges |
| Three | friendliness, helpfulness, information accuracy | compact, badge-ready | no explicit overall cross-check |
| Four | three dimensions plus overall | balanced diagnostic and validity check | more fatigue and straight-lining |
| Five+ | add speed/ease or sector items | richer segmentation | mobile survey burden; unclear badge meaning |

## Recommendation for owner review

Prototype **three and four**. Prefer three for the first data-collection pilot unless comprehension
testing shows the overall question materially improves interpretation. Do not expose badges until
sample adequacy and response-quality checks are validated. A one-question fallback is defensible if
completion or comprehension is poor; it intentionally postpones dimension badges.

## Measurement plan

Compare completion, per-question skip/N/A, median time, abandonment point, identical-answer runs,
response dispersion, contradiction with overall response and customer comprehension. Do not use a
higher completion rate alone as proof of better data.

## Fatigue controls

- One screen or progressive disclosure with clear remaining effort.
- No forced answer, preselection, hidden required state or repeated reminder after skip.
- Ask once per transaction, not once per basket line.
- Avoid sector-specific branching in V1.
- Store question-set version so later changes never relabel historical answers.
