# Customer Rating and Merchant Reputation Separation

Status: **PROPOSED — CUSTOMER RATING REMAINS INDEPENDENT**
Wave: 18 / Workstream AA

## Customer rating

Customer-generated eligible feedback aggregate with visible count, distribution/context and canonical review contract. Merchant cannot edit, delete, hide, buy or outweigh it.

## Merchant reputation

Broader bounded system signals such as factual verification, measured listing accuracy, current policy state and independently governed operational integrity. It must not masquerade as a replacement star rating.

## Display rules

- Always show actual eligible customer rating/count where the product currently exposes it.
- A reputation badge appears separately with “why” explanation and evidence window.
- Insufficient history is explicit; no proprietary score fills the gap.
- Bad rating cannot be hidden behind “trusted” badge, paid campaign or composite score.
- Review correction/deletion follows review policy; reputation engine consumes resulting canonical state without rewriting content.

## Recommendation

Avoid a single merchant reputation number. Prefer visible customer rating plus separate factual badges/statuses. Any composite score requires major owner, fairness, policy and explainability review.
