## Classification rationale

### LIKELY_APPROVE_AS_IS_AFTER_ROOT_RULES

These eight domains retain coherent L2 spines once reusable precedence rules are applied. Their remaining work is an explicit owner ballot, lower-level design, editorial names or policy metadata—not a predicted L2 restructure.

### MINOR_NODE_EDIT_EXPECTED

These three domains appear structurally sound but need a small scope statement, rename or exclusion clarification before approval. No broad redesign is recommended.

### SUBSTANTIVE_EDIT_EXPECTED

These five domains contain a node whose ownership, exception registry or restricted-product scope can materially change the L2 structure. Bulk approval would hide a real choice.

### POLICY_DEPENDENT

These six domains cannot safely convert their root recommendation into a structural approval without keeping a current eligibility/scope gate visible. Policy review is separate from owner taxonomy judgment and remains fail closed.
