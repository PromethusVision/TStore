# Unified Review and Badge Privacy/Policy Audit

**State:** PRIVACY/POLICY QUESTIONS — PROFESSIONAL REVIEW MAY BE REQUIRED

This is product/technical analysis, not legal advice. Final notice, retention and disclosure wording needs
the Wave 24 compliance/professional-review path.

## Data minimization

| Data | Purpose | Public? | Guardrail |
|---|---|---|---|
| customer subject ID | uniqueness/cap/appeal | No | pseudonymous server use |
| verified purchase/item/shop | eligibility/provenance | bounded label only | not payment/receipt claim |
| product review text/rating | product opinion/feed | Yes under policy | moderation/deletion rights model |
| structured values | shop dimension aggregate | individual values optional | no customer identity |
| shop/region | scope/local badge | shop region may be public | no customer GPS |
| fraud signals | integrity review | No | least privilege and appeal-safe reasons |
| aggregate/badge history | explainability/audit | bounded | versioned retention policy |

## Policy requirements

- Explain why product review and shop questions share a purchase but remain separate.
- Publish verification method and apply positive/negative review rules symmetrically.
- Make badge methodology, sample/window meaning and non-guarantee accessible.
- Do not infer sensitive traits or collect precise location for neighborhood ranking.
- Do not export product free text to generic analytics or ad targeting.
- Define deletion/correction effect on public content versus immutable purchase/audit evidence.
- Retention, lawful basis, user rights, automated-decision significance and notices remain professional-review
  items before public scoring/badges.

## Fail-closed items

Public badges wait if method notice, correction/appeal path, fraud controls or privacy review is incomplete.
Structured data may be collected only after purpose/notice/minimization and retention are approved.
