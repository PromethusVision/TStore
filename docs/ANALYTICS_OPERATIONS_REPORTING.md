# EsnaftaVar Operations Reporting

**State:** `PROPOSED — ALIGNED WITH OPERATIONS FOUNDATION`

Operational reporting covers case intake/backlog/age, policy/action outcomes,
appeal/reversal, evidence completeness, restricted-security alerts, QR anomaly
queues, data-quality incidents and response freshness.

Counts are segmented by case/action/policy version and severity, not private
content. “Confirmed” and “reported/suspected” remain distinct. Operator productivity
metrics must not reward unsafe speed or expose sensitive cases; quality, due process
and reversal rates accompany throughput.

Access/export is capability-based and audited. Merchant/customer dashboards do not
receive cross-merchant cases, reporter identity, detection logic or operator notes.
Exact SLA/retention/staffing thresholds require owner/policy decisions.
