# EsnaftaVar Esenler Pilot — Legal and Compliance Dependencies

**State:** `ISSUE CHECKLIST — NOT LEGAL ADVICE OR APPROVAL`

Professional review must be scoped to the actual pilot, data, merchants, messaging,
channels and commercial offer. A small pilot is not exempt by being called a test.

## Pre-launch review areas

- entity/operator identity, customer and merchant terms, pilot/free-offer end state;
- privacy roles, purposes, legal bases, notices, data minimization, retention,
  processors/vendors, rights and deletion interactions;
- precise/coarse location, crash analytics, support evidence and research consent;
- merchant identity/authority evidence and sensitive-document handling;
- commercial advertising accuracy and social/paid channel responsibilities;
- commercial electronic messaging/consent/opt-out where applicable;
- regulated or policy-sensitive product/sector allowlist and required evidence;
- QR wording: verified physical-purchase fact, not payment/invoice/revenue;
- review/moderation/report/appeal process;
- platform-store declarations, accessibility, age/child protections where relevant;
- accounting/tax review before paid subscription, ads or economic reward.

The KVKK describes processing broadly from collection through deletion/anonymization,
and its general principles include specified legitimate purpose, relevance/
proportionality and appropriate retention. The Trade Ministry states commercial
advertising must be honest and accurate. These are review anchors, not case-specific
legal conclusions:

- [KVKK — Kişisel Verilerin İşlenmesi](https://www.kvkk.gov.tr/Icerik/2048/Kisisel-Verilerin-Islenmesi)
- [KVKK — General Principles](https://www.kvkk.gov.tr/Icerik/6606/General-Principles-in-Processing-of-Personal-Data)
- [T.C. Ticaret Bakanlığı — Ticari Reklamlar](https://ticaret.gov.tr/tuketici/ticari-reklamlar)

## Fail-closed gate

If accountable reviewer, evidence, customer/merchant wording, incident route or
retention/access cannot be named for a material risk, exclude the feature/domain/
channel until resolved.
