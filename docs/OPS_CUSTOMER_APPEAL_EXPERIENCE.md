# Customer Appeal Experience

**State:** PROPOSED — NO UI OR POLICY FINALIZATION

## Experience

1. Show affected object/action and plain-language safe reason.
2. Distinguish temporary hold, rejection, restriction, removal, and technical unavailability.
3. Offer “request review” for owner-approved material decisions.
4. Authenticate through canonical session/recovery; never ask password/OTP/link.
5. Collect structured contested reason and minimal new evidence.
6. Show appeal reference/status and evidence request.
7. Communicate outcome, effective scope, restoration/superseding action, and further route if any.

## States

`ELIGIBLE`, `SUBMITTED`, `NEEDS_INFORMATION`, `IN_REVIEW`, `UPHELD`, `MODIFIED`, `OVERTURNED`, `CLOSED`. Duplicate submissions link; they do not reset priority or create punishment.

## Trust safeguards

Do not expose reporter identity/fraud methods, shame the user, imply guilt before review, punish appeal, require unnecessary documents, or promise exact result/time. Preserve access to ordinary support and emergency/security reporting.

## Accessibility/privacy

Localized readable copy, accessible status, safe attachment path if supported, data-minimized form, clear retention/visibility, and non-public case reference. Email notifications link to authenticated detail.
