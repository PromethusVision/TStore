# Store Review Risk

**State:** GENERAL RISK ANALYSIS — NO APPROVAL GUARANTEE

Potential review risks include incomplete privacy declarations, permission purpose mismatch, broken login/recovery/deep links, unavailable reviewer access, misleading screenshots/copy, unsupported commerce claims, user-generated content/reporting gaps, account deletion/support discoverability, hidden debug/demo behavior and unstable backend availability.

## Mitigation

- align submitted binary, metadata, privacy/support URLs and actual behavior;
- provide safe reviewer instructions/accounts without reusable Production secrets;
- explain location/camera/QR purpose accurately;
- test external network, confirmation and recovery from the submitted artifact;
- preserve review correspondence and respond with evidence;
- never promise approval date or bypass store policy.

Store rules can change and require current official review near submission.

OWNER_DECISION_REQUIRED: assign store metadata/policy owner and reviewer-support process.
