## Daily minimum

1. review health and exact release/environment;
2. reconcile QR and critical authoritative events;
3. review stale/disputed listings and candidate queue;
4. inspect P0/P1 and unowned/aged support cases;
5. review acquisition source quality, not only volume;
6. contact no customer/merchant outside approved support/feedback process;
7. record one factual daily decision: continue, hold, narrow or pause.

| Day | Focus | Expansion condition |

## Week-one scorecard

Active ready shops/listings, cell/domain density, qualified first use, search/no-
result, product/shop/directions intent, QR attempts/confirmed/rejections/coverage,
support cases/age/severity, crashes/critical RPC, listing truth corrections,
merchant/customer feedback and pauses. Verified purchases are not revenue.

## Week-two choices

Continue unchanged, repair without growth, narrow cell/domain/channel, expand one
bounded dimension, pause QR while retaining discovery, or pause the pilot. No option
is selected automatically by a vanity threshold.
