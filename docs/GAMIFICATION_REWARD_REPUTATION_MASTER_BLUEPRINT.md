# Reward, Gamification and Reputation Master Blueprint

**State:** EXECUTIVE ENTRY POINT — NOT CANONICAL

Labels used: **PROPOSED**, **RECOMMENDED**, **TBD**, **OWNER_DECISION_REQUIRED**. Nothing in this blueprint is Product Owner final.

## 1. Reward Engine purpose

**PROPOSED:** optional economic/value-oriented loyalty based on legitimate local physical commerce. It is not review eligibility, customer reputation, advertising or a coupon engine by assumption.

**RECOMMENDED:** do not launch economic reward in the first Esenler commercial pilot.

## 2. Ödül Çubuğu options

Purchase count/stamps, spend weighting, points, merchant progress, platform progress and hybrid were compared. **RECOMMENDED:** if revisited post-pilot, test non-economic merchant-specific fixed-stamp/count shadow progress. Numeric formula/threshold is **TBD**.

## 3. Trusted earning evidence

Only a unique server-authoritative merchant-confirmed verified physical purchase can be a candidate. Client/cart/view/directions/wishlist/ad events never award economic value. Repeat and quantity semantics are **OWNER_DECISION_REQUIRED** and never create extra review rights. Monetary authority is unproven.

## 4. Funding options

Merchant, platform and hybrid funding remain **OWNER_DECISION_REQUIRED**. Bounded merchant-funded templates are the least risky later hypothesis; professional accounting/tax/legal review precedes issuance.

## 5. Redemption and expiry

Discount, voucher, benefit and non-economic unlock remain options. Expiry must be prospective, fixed/versioned, disclosed and auditable. **RECOMMENDED:** no economic redemption until reconciliation/support exist.

## 6. Fraud model

P0 threats include forged/replayed/concurrent QR, collusion, self-purchase, quantity/amount inflation, privileged edits and policy bypass. Controls use atomic source confirmation, idempotency, immutable linked corrections, anomaly holds and human appeal.

## 7. Customer badge model

Badge families are **PROPOSED FOR OWNER REVIEW**. **RECOMMENDED:** post-pilot, private/opt-in, explainable badges from strong events only. No social-credit score or sensitive-purchase inference.

## 8. Levels, challenges and streaks

**RECOMMENDED:** no levels; defer challenges; do not use purchase streaks. Discovery challenges need evidence and dark-pattern review before reconsideration.

## 9. Merchant reputation model

**RECOMMENDED:** post-pilot shop-first factual signals with sample size/freshness and `insufficient history`; no composite score. Verified volume is activity confidence, not quality proof.

## 10. Merchant badge model

Families and names are **PROPOSED**, not final. Any later badge requires strong evidence, clear scope, lifecycle/revocation, fairness and merchant appeal. It is not a guarantee.

## 11. New-merchant fairness

No-history is neutral, not low trust. Ads/reward participation cannot lift trust. Cross-sector comparisons and service reputation remain deferred.

## 12. Review/rating separation

The confirmed review contract is unchanged: merchant-confirmed QR purchase unlocks one active review per customer + canonical product for life; repeat/quantity adds no right; immutable evidence survives delete/recreate. Customer rating/count remains visible and cannot be hidden by reputation or merchant dispute.

## 13. Ads/Reputation separation

Advertising may buy labeled visibility only. Spend/impression/click/attribution is excluded from reward, badge and reputation evidence. A sponsored purchase is treated like an equivalent organic verified purchase under any future approved reward policy.

## 14. Policy-sensitive boundaries

Alcohol, tobacco, medicines, health services, supplements/health claims, infant products, medical devices, hunting/weapons, pyrotechnics and regulated chemicals fail closed pending current legal/policy review.

## 15. V1 vs future

**MUST_HAVE:** core verified purchase/review/ads invariants and measurements. **SHOULD_HAVE:** non-public coverage/fairness analytics. **DEFER:** economic reward, public badges/reputation, levels/challenges/streaks.

## 16. Root owner decisions

There are 40 raw decisions, 16 semantic clusters and 16 root decisions (12 P0, 3 P1, 1 P2). Review RGR-01–RGR-10 first. Options are simulated; none selected.

## 17. Implementation sequence

Owner decisions → professional reviews → stable event/identity contracts → immutable ledger/events → fraud/idempotency/auth → Development shadow mode → flagged surfaces → automated stress/load/security → controlled observation → explicit Production decision.
