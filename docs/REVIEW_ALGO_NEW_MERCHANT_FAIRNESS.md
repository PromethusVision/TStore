# New Merchant Fairness Model

**State:** FAIRNESS REQUIREMENTS — THRESHOLDS OPEN

## Principle

No history is not bad history. A new shop is discoverable under normal organic eligibility and is
labeled `Yeni / yeterli değerlendirme yok`; it receives neither a zero score nor an unearned quality
badge.

## Safeguards

- Badge eligibility waits for sample, unique-customer, freshness and integrity gates.
- Search must not create a permanent cold-start trap by using badges as a hard organic prerequisite.
- New merchants can display factual verified attributes independently of reputation.
- Paid promotion cannot substitute for history or move a merchant across reputation gates.
- A missing response is not imputed as neutral or negative.
- Pilot cohort or shop category can be used for analysis, but hidden peer-group priors require audit.

## Candidate status progression

`NO_HISTORY → DEVELOPING_HISTORY → ELIGIBLE_FOR_EVALUATION → BADGE_DECISION`

This is a data-readiness progression, not a merchant-quality ladder. A shop may remain developing
because of low purchase volume without penalty.

## Fair exposure options

1. Organic ranking reserves non-badge discovery paths.
2. Badge filters default off and explain insufficient history.
3. Internal dashboards monitor exposure and evaluation acquisition by shop age/volume.
4. Any future exploration mechanism is bounded and separate from reputation truth.
