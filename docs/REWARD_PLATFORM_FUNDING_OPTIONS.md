# Platform-Funded Reward Options

**State:** OPTIONS — OWNER DECISION REQUIRED

Platform-funded value can support cross-merchant discovery but concentrates financial, accounting, fraud and customer-support liability.

## Candidates

- Fixed capped pilot campaign: explicit budget, population and deadline.
- Category/neighborhood discovery campaign: only ordinary allowlisted goods; avoid spending pressure.
- Service recovery credit: human-approved support remedy, not gamification.
- Permanent platform currency: not recommended without mature accounting/settlement infrastructure.

Every campaign needs budget reservation, eligibility version, duplicate/reversal handling, exhausted-budget behavior and immutable customer terms. Budget exhaustion cannot invalidate already-earned value. Policy-sensitive purchases and ad clicks/views are ineligible.

**Recommendation:** defer permanent platform-funded value; consider only a separately approved, capped post-pilot experiment after professional review.
