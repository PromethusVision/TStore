# Merchant App Review Management Model

Status: **PROPOSED — FROZEN REVIEW CONTRACT PRESERVED**
Wave: 17 / WP45

## Merchant capabilities

- View customer-visible eligible reviews for authorized shop/product context.
- Filter by date, rating and response/report status when supported.
- Report a review through governed reason classes.
- See aggregate rating/count consistent with Customer App.

## Merchant cannot

- Edit, delete, hide, reorder or relabel a customer review.
- Create review evidence or grant a customer review rights.
- Infer/receive unnecessary customer identity.
- Retaliate through catalog, service or staff tooling.
- Treat legacy boolean verification as verified evidence.

## Frozen entitlement

Only merchant-confirmed QR physical purchase unlocks review; one active review per customer + canonical product for life. Repeat purchase/quantity grants no extra active review. Delete/recreate relies on immutable verified evidence.

## V1 recommendation

Read-only review inbox plus safe reporting. Merchant replies remain an owner decision and may be deferred.
