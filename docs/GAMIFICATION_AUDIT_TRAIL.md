# Gamification Audit Trail

**State:** CONCEPTUAL — RETENTION REVIEW REQUIRED

Every economic or public-trust change must answer: what changed, why, from which immutable evidence/policy version, for which stable scope, when, by which authorized actor/system, under which idempotency key and what it superseded/reversed.

## Audited actions

- Reward earn, adjust, reverse, redeem, expire and settlement state.
- Badge earn, activate, revoke, retire and supersede.
- Merchant signal inclusion/exclusion, evidence window and badge lifecycle.
- Policy/config version changes, fraud holds, human decisions and disputes.
- Replay/backfill/reconciliation runs and privileged access.

Logs are append-only, access-controlled, tamper-evident and privacy-minimized. Public explanations expose meaning/evidence summary, not internal fraud thresholds or personal purchase detail. Exact retention needs legal/privacy/accounting review.
