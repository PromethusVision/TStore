### OM-R07=B — Product / domain-gated Variant / Listing identity

The canonical catalog identity direction is:

- `PRODUCT` as canonical product identity;
- `VARIANT` only where a domain genuinely requires a distinct variant identity;
- separate `SHOP LISTING` for merchant-owned commercial truth;
- identifiers treated as evidence, not blindly authoritative identity.

Variant is not mandatory for every domain or product. Price, availability/stock
and merchant SKU remain listing-owned. Barcode/GTIN may support identity evidence
but cannot silently define or merge canonical identity. No runtime or schema
implementation is authorized.
