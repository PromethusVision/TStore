# Esenler Minimum Commercial Pilot

**State:** RECOMMENDED PILOT SHAPE — NOT OWNER APPROVED

## Customer promise

People can discover real nearby shops/listings, compare seller truth, build a
single-shop cart, present a short-lived QR at that shop, receive a
server-authoritative verified purchase and write at most one active review per
canonical product. No online payment/shipping promise is introduced.

## Minimum operating system

1. **Customer App:** current functional O2O core; no new large module.
2. **Catalog:** owner-final Product Taxonomy V1 plus validated active products,
   shops and listings; explicit demo provenance.
3. **Merchant path:** authenticated exact-shop verifier and bounded listing truth.
   It may be a controlled pilot tool/process before the full Merchant App.
4. **Backend:** current Customer contracts preserved; exact-shop authorization,
   one-winner QR and review evidence remain authoritative.
5. **Operations:** named support/security contact, manual case/evidence log,
   escalation/containment/cleanup runbook and regulated/unknown fail-closed path.
6. **Observability:** startup/Auth/RPC/QR errors, release/environment identity,
   privacy-safe health counts and daily reconciliation.
7. **QA/release:** immutable signed artifact, physical permissions/deep links/GPS,
   two-device QR, backend compatibility and owner go/no-go.

## Explicitly absent

- Ads or sponsored ranking;
- Reward/points/vouchers;
- levels, challenges or streaks;
- public merchant reputation/badges;
- online checkout/payment/shipping;
- enterprise merchant organization/roles;
- behavioral personalization or precise-location history;
- automated moderation/appeals platform;
- complete iOS launch unless owner chooses it as a pilot gate.

## Pilot success questions

- Can customers reliably find relevant local offers and complete physical QR?
- Can merchants keep essential listing truth and verify purchases safely?
- Does review evidence remain trustworthy?
- Are crashes/Auth/QR/support issues observable and recoverable?
- Is operational workload sustainable without advanced systems?
