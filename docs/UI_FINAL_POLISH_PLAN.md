# UI Final Polish Plan

## Purpose

Final polish is a bounded defect-closing phase after all screen families have
functional and structural acceptance. It is not a second redesign.

## Polish passes

1. **Hierarchy:** primary CTA, price, product/shop identity and recovery action.
2. **Rhythm:** section spacing, card density, edge alignment and scroll endpoints.
3. **Typography:** role consistency, Turkish line breaks and numeric alignment.
4. **Media:** aspect ratios, fallback consistency and loading transitions.
5. **State cues:** disabled/focus/error/success/availability/trust beyond color.
6. **Motion:** restrained transitions, duplicate motion removal, reduced-motion.
7. **Platform:** status/navigation bars, keyboard, safe areas and back behavior.
8. **Accessibility:** semantics, focus, contrast, 2.0 text and one-hand targets.
9. **Content:** developer copy, annotations, terminology and punctuation.
10. **Cross-screen consistency:** repeated component and message comparisons.

## Pilot-critical before launch

- Home/listing/product/seller/shop/Cart/AuthGuard hierarchy and states.
- Readable labels, prices, availability and physical-shop actions.
- Correct C1 items and no developer annotation/copy residue.
- Consistent loading/empty/error and image fallbacks.
- Touch, contrast, text-scale and exact artifact checks.

## Safe post-pilot polish

- decorative transitions and advanced hero motion;
- dark mode if owner explicitly chooses a light-only pilot and release contract
  supports it safely;
- low-traffic legal/help decorative refinement;
- optional tablet-specific composition beyond safe max-width behavior;
- dormant ads/reward/gamification visuals;
- Merchant-adjacent visual alignment;
- micro-level shadow/icon variations with no comprehension impact.

## Stop rule

After visual freeze, only V0/V1 defects or explicitly approved release-critical
changes enter the artifact. V2/V3 ideas move to post-pilot backlog to prevent
commercialization delay through endless polish.
