# Esenler Pilot Compliance Minimum

**State:** PROPOSED FOR PROFESSIONAL AND PRODUCT-OWNER REVIEW
**Scope:** First commercial Esenler pilot; this is not legal advice.

## Launch-blocking minimum

| Gate | Minimum evidence before launch | Decision/reviewer |
|---|---|---|
| Platform role | Released capabilities and factual role description reconciled with counsel; no claim that EsnaftaVar is seller, payment provider, carrier or receipt issuer | `LAWYER` |
| Privacy notice | Activity-specific Turkish notice covers actual Production data flows, controller contact, purposes, recipients, collection method/ground and rights route | `KVKK/PRIVACY_SPECIALIST` |
| Data minimization | Exact location is transient; raw search history, behavioral ad profile and unnecessary device identifiers are off | `PRODUCT_OWNER` + privacy review |
| Rights route | Reachable access/correction/deletion/objection route, identity-safe intake and auditable fulfilment exist | `KVKK/PRIVACY_SPECIALIST` |
| Account deletion | Auth, profile, Storage, vendors, backups and retained evidence have a tested orchestration rule | `TECHNICAL_ARCHITECT` + privacy review |
| Security | Least privilege, secret hygiene, breach triage and KVKK notification decision workflow exist | `TECHNICAL_ARCHITECT` + privacy review |
| Merchant baseline | Ordinary identity/shop-existence evidence, status scope and recheck trigger are defined | `PRODUCT_OWNER` |
| Regulated inventory | Pilot exclusions and fail-closed review routing are enforced at item/capability level | `DOMAIN_REGULATORY_SPECIALIST` |
| Price/availability | Merchant source and observation/update time are visible; platform does not promise availability | `PRODUCT_OWNER` + `LAWYER` |
| QR | Customer sees that verification is not payment, invoice, fiscal receipt or audited revenue proof | `PRODUCT_OWNER` + `LAWYER` |
| Reviews | Verified-review method, parity, objective moderation, reporting and appeal rules are public | `LAWYER` + `PRODUCT_OWNER` |
| Support | A reachable contact/complaint channel and internal escalation owner exist | `PRODUCT_OWNER` |
| Mobile declarations | Store privacy/Data Safety answers match the exact release artifact and Production flows | `PRODUCT_OWNER` + privacy review |

## Feature-dependent gates

- Sponsored placement may remain disabled. If enabled, `Sponsorlu` disclosure, explanation,
  contextual-only targeting, evidence-backed claims and separate ad eligibility must pass.
- Rewards may remain disabled. Economic or cross-merchant value must not launch before legal,
  payment-services and accounting/tax review.
- Chat/media may be excluded from the pilot. If enabled, access, report, retention and deletion
  contracts become launch gates.
- Regulated product groups can remain excluded while ordinary categories launch. A taxonomy node
  does not authorize sale, listing or advertising.

## Pilot acceptance evidence

1. Exact release-feature inventory and data-flow register.
2. Professional-review queue disposition or explicit fail-closed exclusion.
3. Published/customer-visible texts versioned against the release.
4. Synthetic privacy, merchant, QR, review and policy acceptance results.
5. Manual checks for real support reachability and the exact mobile artifact.

## Not required for the first pilot

An enterprise GRC suite, broad behavioral advertising, transferable rewards, automated legal
classification, all-category regulated onboarding and a large moderation team are not prerequisites
when the related capability is disabled and the remaining minimum controls are real.
