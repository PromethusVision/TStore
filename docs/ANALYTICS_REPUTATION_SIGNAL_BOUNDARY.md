# EsnaftaVar Reputation Signal Boundary

**State:** `PROPOSED SAFETY BOUNDARY — SCORE/POLICY NOT FINAL`

Reputation is a derived, versioned projection of governed evidence—not a mutable
merchant field and not a generic engagement score.

Potential evidence classes include active eligible reviews, verified-purchase
volume/quality bands, upheld trust-and-safety outcomes and catalog/operational
reliability. Each source declares authority, confidence, lookback, appeal/reversal
behavior and anti-gaming rules before use.

Excluded as reputation evidence by default:

- paid impressions, ad clicks, campaign spend or sponsored placement;
- raw page views, wishlist/cart/directions intent;
- unverified complaints, private chat sentiment or operator free text;
- demographic, precise location or protected/sensitive inferences;
- reward participation or gamification points.

Source corrections recompute projections through lineage; operators act on source
cases/evidence rather than directly editing a score. Merchant-facing explanations
must disclose material factor classes without exposing reporter/customer identity
or detection secrets.

No reputation score, weight, threshold or consequence is selected here.
