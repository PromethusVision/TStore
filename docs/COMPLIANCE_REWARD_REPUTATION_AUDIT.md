# Reward, Gamification and Reputation Compliance Audit

**State:** OPTIONS/QUESTIONS — NO ECONOMIC OR TAX MODEL APPROVED
**Advice status:** not legal, accounting or tax advice

## V1 recommendation

Do not promise economic value in the first Esenler pilot. Non-economic progress or
education mechanics may be considered only if they do not mislead, pressure reviews,
reveal sensitive behavior or create a paid reputation advantage. Any point, voucher,
discount entitlement, cashback-like value or transferable credit waits for legal,
accounting/tax, privacy and product approval.

## Economic-value questions

| Question | Why it matters | Required reviewer |
|---|---|---|
| Who owes the benefit: platform or merchant? | customer remedy, liability and reconciliation | `LAWYER` + `ACCOUNTANT/TAX_ADVISER` |
| When is value earned? | QR confirmation, correction and dispute change obligation | owner + accountant |
| Is value fixed, variable or revocable? | trust, unfair term and accounting exposure | lawyer + accountant |
| Can it be spent at another merchant? | clearing/value-transfer/payment-regulation risk | `LAWYER` with payments expertise |
| Can it be transferred, gifted or converted to cash? | stronger payment/e-money risk | lawyer/payments specialist |
| Who funds discount and how is it invoiced? | VAT/income/expense/document treatment | `ACCOUNTANT/TAX_ADVISER` |
| What happens on refund/correction/account closure? | append-only reversal and customer rights | lawyer + accountant |
| Can points expire? | disclosure, fairness, outstanding obligation | lawyer + owner + accountant |
| What records are kept? | reconciliation, tax/audit and privacy | accountant + privacy specialist |

## Cross-merchant value

Cross-merchant earning/redemption is `BLOCKED_PENDING_PROFESSIONAL_REVIEW`. Do not
represent points as money, hold funds, calculate merchant settlement or create a
transferable balance before scope and licensing conclusions. A “closed-loop” label
does not decide the law; network, scale, funding, redemption and acceptance facts do.

## Regulated-product exclusion

No reward, progress, near-threshold message, challenge, voucher, discount, streak or
badge is driven by alcohol, tobacco/nicotine, medicines, infant formula, regulated
health claims, weapons, pyrotechnics, pesticides, or another product marked
`EXCLUDED`/`LEGAL_REVIEW_REQUIRED`. For mixed purchases, reward only exact eligible
items when item-level evidence exists; otherwise issue nothing and preserve the
reason.

## Ledger and disclosure requirements if later approved

- append-only `EARN`, `ADJUST`, `REVERSE`, `REDEEM`, `EXPIRE` facts;
- program/funder/rule/policy/eligibility snapshot on every entry;
- no balance overwrite or retroactive rule substitution;
- customer-visible value, conditions, funder, expiry and correction status;
- reconciliation by merchant/program and dispute-ready evidence;
- issuance kill switch that does not erase earned rights;
- account-closure and deletion treatment approved in advance;
- separate accounting records, marketing analytics and abuse signals.

## Reputation/badge claims

| Candidate badge | Minimum evidence | Prohibited implication |
|---|---|---|
| Merchant verified | exact current identity/shop/capability scope | government approval or all-products authorization |
| QR participating | active server-authoritative capability | every sale/price verified |
| Review quality | disclosed eligible-review statistic | paid boost or subjective platform guarantee |
| Responsive merchant | defined response denominator/window | superior product or legal compliance |
| Long-standing | verified start/continuity rule | absence of complaints or trust guarantee |

Advertising spend never raises reputation. Reviews/reputation cannot be conditioned on
a positive rating. Operators cannot manually increase scores; correction is append-only,
evidence-backed and audited. Public badges do not reveal customer health, alcohol,
tobacco, infant-care or other sensitive purchases.

## Expiry and customer trust

If economic value is ever approved, expiry must be prominent before earning, stable
under the applicable version, communicated proportionately and reversible when an
operational error caused loss. Silence, buried terms or surprise expiry is not an
acceptable pilot model. Exact period remains an owner/professional decision.
