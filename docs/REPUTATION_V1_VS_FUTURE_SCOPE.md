# Merchant Reputation V1 vs Future Scope

**State:** RECOMMENDED — NOT OWNER APPROVED

## Pilot

Keep customer rating average/count visible and independent. Do not launch a composite merchant-reputation score or badges before enough verified-purchase/review history and branch identity exist.

## Post-pilot candidates

- Explainable factual signals: verified activity confidence, rating sample size, listing/availability accuracy and resolved QR abuse status.
- `Insufficient history` for new merchants instead of a low-trust label.
- Shop-scoped evidence first; merchant-org aggregation only after owner and identity decisions.
- Badge candidates only when evidence, freshness, revocation and appeal are documented.

## Deferred

- Proprietary all-in-one score, cross-sector ranking and product-specific quality score.
- Service-quality reputation until a trustworthy service-completion event exists.
- Responsiveness signal until communication/SLA semantics exist.
- Any paid, sponsored or reward-program signal.

**Recommendation:** `POST_PILOT` shadow monitoring, then limited factual signals; not a guarantee of quality.
