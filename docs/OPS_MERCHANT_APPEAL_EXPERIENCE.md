# Merchant Appeal Experience

**State:** PROPOSED — NO MERCHANT UI OR POLICY FINALIZATION

## Covered decisions

Verification denial/expiry, listing/media/review moderation, policy restriction, QR capability/fraud hold, merchant/shop suspension, catalog candidate decision, and ads/reward decisions if later enabled.

## Flow

Authenticated organization/shop scope → decision detail → safe reason and affected dependencies → structured appeal/new evidence → higher-assurance check for ownership/sensitive documents → status/evidence request → independent review where possible → outcome/restoration/recheck → audit.

## Merchant information

Show exact affected object, current state, rule/effective version, required correction/evidence category, whether other eligible operations remain, review/expiry timing category, and appeal ID. Do not reveal reporter/customer identity, competitor data, fraud thresholds, device/IP fingerprints, internal notes, or operator personal details.

## Boundaries

Appeal cannot buy priority through spend, change canonical facts without evidence, delete negative reviews, mark QR verified, set reputation, grant regulated permission, or guarantee ad delivery. A corrected listing/evidence is a new version and may require review.

## One-person pilot

Record independence gap, use structured evidence/policy checks, and require retrospective sample for high-risk outcomes. Broad/permanent action remains owner/policy gated.
