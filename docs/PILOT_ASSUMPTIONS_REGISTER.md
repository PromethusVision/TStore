# EsnaftaVar Esenler Pilot — Assumptions Register

**State:** `WORKING ASSUMPTIONS — VALIDATE BEFORE COMMITMENT`

Ranges below are planning bands, not forecasts, promises or approved targets.

| ID | Working assumption | Why needed | Validation evidence | If false |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| PA-01 | Pilot begins in one or a few contiguous Esenler micro-zones, not district-wide. | Concentrates supply and field support. | Merchant map plus customer travel tests. | Shrink or change the launch cell. |
| PA-02 | Very lean supply is `12–25` launch-ready shops; moderate is `30–60`; broader is `75–120`. | Enables workload scenarios. | Signed-up, verified and active shop counts. | Recalculate staffing/acquisition; never call count alone sufficient. |
| PA-03 | A launch-ready shop has roughly `15–60` useful current listings, with category-specific exceptions. | Avoids a logo-only directory. | Listing audit and customer task completion. | Add bootstrap help or narrow categories. |
| PA-04 | Each launch cell needs several independently useful merchants in its promised domains. | A single merchant does not offer meaningful discovery. | Supply-gap heatmap. | Do not market that domain/cell broadly. |
| PA-05 | Ordinary low-policy retail domains launch before regulated or service-heavy domains. | Reduces verification/legal burden. | Owner-approved allowlist and evidence matrix. | Pause affected domain. |
| PA-06 | Merchant self-onboarding alone will not fill the first catalog. | Early merchants need assisted setup. | Funnel and assistance-time data. | Expand self-service only after evidence. |
| PA-07 | One trained operator plus owner escalation can handle only a tightly controlled pilot. | Lean staffing hypothesis. | Daily case arrival, age and interruption log. | Cap onboarding, add coverage or pause growth. |
| PA-08 | Support is declared-hours, not 24/7. | Prevents false service promise. | Published channel/hours and incident rota. | Reduce feature scope if critical coverage is absent. |
| PA-09 | Customer launch starts invitation/closed-track before broad visibility. | Produces safer learning and exact-artifact control. | Cohort acceptance and incident data. | Delay public acquisition. |
| PA-10 | Android can cover the first controlled cohort. | iOS commercial readiness is incomplete. | Target-customer device survey and exclusions log. | Include iOS only after its gates pass. |
| PA-11 | Meta can be one acquisition test, not the sole channel or guaranteed converter. | Local reach hypothesis. | Holdout/source-tagged qualified use. | Rebalance to field/referral/merchant channels. |
| PA-12 | Organic merchant-owned and local-community channels can recruit early users. | Low-cost acquisition option. | Source-coded installs and completed discovery tasks. | Stop ineffective channel; do not inflate vanity reach. |
| PA-13 | QR is teachable at point of sale with a short merchant script. | Enables verified purchase/review evidence. | Observed scan/confirm success and support cases. | Simplify training or run pre-QR phase. |
| PA-14 | Availability is truth-as-known, not perfect real-time stock. | No inventory integration exists. | Freshness timestamps, disputes and merchant updates. | Tighten expiry/unknown behavior. |
| PA-15 | Ads are unnecessary to prove organic discovery. | Avoids premature billing/policy load. | Organic pilot learning. | Revisit post-stabilization, not by assumption. |
| PA-16 | Rewards/gamification are unnecessary for initial retention learning. | Prevents subsidized or distorted behavior. | Natural repeat-use and interview evidence. | Test shadow concepts later; do not promise value. |
| PA-17 | Verified-purchase coverage will be incomplete. | Not every physical purchase will use QR. | QR attempts versus merchant-observed eligible interactions. | Label coverage; never infer total sales. |
| PA-18 | Customer and merchant feedback can be collected through a minimal governed channel. | Lean learning loop. | Consent, response and resolution audit. | Remove unused channel or add bounded assistance. |
| PA-19 | Existing provider logs plus one privacy-reviewed crash/error tool can support the pilot minimum. | Avoids enterprise tooling before need. | Alert drills and incident reconstruction. | Add tooling only for a demonstrated gap. |
| PA-20 | No financial outcome or conversion rate is predictable before field evidence. | Protects decision quality. | Pilot baseline and cohorts. | Keep using ranges and confidence labels. |

## Numerical-assumption rule

Every numerical band is revisited at each readiness checkpoint. It becomes a
target only through an explicit owner decision with cost, capacity and measurement
evidence. A range that appears in a scenario CSV is not approval.
