**State:** `PROPOSED FUNNEL OPTIONS — NOT FINAL`

shop verification completed, approved, first catalog-ready listing, first QR
capability, first authoritative verified purchase and sustained active status.

CRM/sales outreach, merchant app and platform facts use stable merchant/shop IDs
with source provenance; duplicate leads merge without losing source history. Counts
separate pending, rejected, inactive and policy-blocked merchants.

Acquisition cost/quality requires complete spend and approved activation/retention
definitions. Staff productivity must not reward unsafe approvals. Merchant contact
and case detail are restricted; dashboards use aggregates.
