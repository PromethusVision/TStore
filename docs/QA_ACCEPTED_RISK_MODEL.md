# Accepted Risk Model

**State:** PROPOSED — NO RISK ACCEPTED HERE

Accepted risk is an explicit exception, not a silent skipped test.

## Required record

- risk and supporting evidence;
- affected customers/merchants/systems/versions;
- likelihood/impact/uncertainty and worst credible outcome;
- why immediate correction is disproportionate or unavailable;
- containment, workaround and monitoring;
- accountable owner and approver;
- release/environment scope;
- expiry or objective revisit trigger;
- planned correction and rollback/escalation.

P0 security, active privacy breach, data corruption, role escalation, false verified purchase, wrong signing/environment and missing Production authority cannot be accepted as routine pilot risk. A changed artifact or new evidence reopens review.

OWNER_DECISION_REQUIRED: define who may accept P2/P3 commercial risk; P0/P1 exceptions require explicit high-risk review.
