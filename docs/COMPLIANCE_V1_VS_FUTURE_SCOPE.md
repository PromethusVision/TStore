# Compliance V1 Versus Future Scope

**State:** PROPOSED SCOPING — NOT OWNER FINAL

## MUST for first pilot

- Exact role/feature inventory, privacy notice and rights contact.
- Transient location/minimal analytics; no undeclared optional processing.
- Merchant baseline, regulated exclusions and policy escalation.
- Price/availability provenance; bounded QR and verified-review explanations.
- Support, moderation/reporting, deletion, retention and breach procedures.
- Store privacy declarations matched to the exact artifact.

## SHOULD after pilot proof

- Formal vendor/transfer register automation, periodic merchant recheck, structured policy-version
  workflow, operator access reviews and aggregate compliance metrics.
- Contextual sponsored placements only after the advertising gate closes.

## DEFER until triggered

- Behavioral ads, cross-app tracking, transferable/cash-like rewards, broad regulated-category
  onboarding, automated legal classification, advanced moderation automation and enterprise GRC.

Disabled capabilities have no launch claim. Re-enabling one reopens its professional and technical
dependencies.
