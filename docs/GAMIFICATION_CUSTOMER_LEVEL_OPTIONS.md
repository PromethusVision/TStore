# Customer Level Options

Status: **PROPOSED — OWNER_DECISION_REQUIRED**
Wave: 18 / Workstream Q

## Options

| Option | Benefit | Risk | Pilot posture |
|---|---|---|---|
| No levels | Simpler, no social hierarchy | Less persistent progression | RECOMMENDED |
| Private personal levels | Long-term progression | Spending proxy and unclear meaning | POST-PILOT RESEARCH |
| Public levels | Visible status/community | Social pressure, privacy, social-credit interpretation | NOT RECOMMENDED |
| Domain-specific mastery | More explainable context | Taxonomy churn and complexity | DEFER |

## If levels are ever approved

- Progress rules must not require frequent spending or positive reviews.
- No public customer ranking/leaderboard.
- Level does not affect service, price, review weight or merchant treatment.
- Evidence, thresholds, decay and correction are explained and versioned.
- Inactivity cannot reduce access or punish the customer.

## Recommendation

No level system in initial Reward/Gamification V1. Use explainable optional badges only after evidence/privacy research. This is an agent recommendation, not a final decision.
