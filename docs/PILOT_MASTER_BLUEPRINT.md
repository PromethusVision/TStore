# EsnaftaVar Esenler Commercial Pilot — Master Blueprint

**State:** `OWNER-REVIEW BLUEPRINT — NOT LAUNCH APPROVAL`

## Executive recommendation

Start smaller than a district rollout: one owner-selected Esenler micro-cell,
roughly `12–25` launch-ready shops as a planning band, `3–5` ordinary product
domains with useful local depth, an invite/closed Android-first customer cohort, a
controlled exact-shop merchant operating path, bounded assisted catalog bootstrap,
QR in a trained limited cohort or staged after discovery acceptance, and one trained
operator with launch backup and named specialist escalation.

Do not launch Ads, economic Reward/Gamification or composite merchant reputation.
Do not wait for 100 merchant registrations, district-wide catalog completeness,
full Merchant App, iOS parity or legacy taxonomy migration if the controlled path's
safety gates pass. These are recommendations, not owner selections.

## Operating architecture

| Plane | Minimum first-pilot contract |
|---|---|
| Product promise | Nearby physical-product discovery; no payment/shipping/checkout/order |
| Geography | Explicit launch cell and honest limited-coverage copy |
| Supply | Verified exact shops, useful ordinary-domain density, current listings |
| Merchant | Risk-tiered verification, controlled/assisted onboarding, exact-shop authority |
| Catalog | Canonical-first reuse, provenance candidates, no auto-publish, stable lineage |
| QR | Server-authoritative one-winner fact; not payment/revenue; daily reconciliation |
| Customer | Guest discovery, login for protected actions, contextual location/manual fallback |
| Acquisition | Merchant/community/referral first; bounded Meta only after gates |
| Support | Declared hours, one queue/operator, backup and named P0/P1 specialists |
| Monitoring | Health/business separation, environment/release identity, low-cost actionable signals |
| Release | One exact signed artifact and physical two-device evidence |
| Governance | Owner decisions, allowlist, terms/privacy, pause and one-dimension expansion |

## Business-model comparison

- **Very lean:** best for workflow/trust proof; high manual share and selection bias.
- **Moderate:** tests repeatability; needs backup, self-service and queue metrics.
- **Broader Esenler:** only after repeatable density, support, release and acquisition
  economics; highest truth-decay and premature-spend risk.

Organic + an explicit later merchant-offer hypothesis is the cleanest first test.
Ads-enabled and Reward/Gamification-enabled modes are separate later experiments.
No financial or conversion outcome is guaranteed.

## Minimum KPI set

Launch-ready shops, current useful listings, usable cell-domain pairs, qualified
first use, search/discovery success, product/shop/directions intent, distinct
verified physical purchases, QR coverage/failure mix, listing truth, review
integrity, critical health, support/P0-P1 health, merchant continuation, customer
useful return and candidate burden. Views/directions/cart are intent; QR is neither
payment nor audited revenue.

## Decision and control system

- 45 raw owner decisions reconcile to 18 roots; first 15 are carded.
- No option is selected in this architecture.
- Launch expands one dimension at a time.
- Any cross-shop/role issue, wrong artifact/environment, duplicate/wrong-shop QR,
  unsafe policy exposure, missing critical owner or misleading supply is a stop.
- Recovery requires evidence, not elapsed time or a quiet dashboard.
