# EsnaftaVar Esenler Pilot — Commercialization Critical Path

**State:** `DEPENDENCY PATH — NO LAUNCH AUTHORIZATION`

## Critical path

1. **Owner closes pilot roots:** mission, cell/density, cohort, allowlist, merchant
   offer/path, QR timing, customer journey, platform, support, terms/privacy and
   pause/expansion.
2. **Field inventory:** verify actual merchants, devices, products, geography,
   support routes and customer travel tasks; replace planning assumptions.
3. **Policy/legal/commercial preflight:** approve actual ordinary-domain allowlist,
   merchant/customer terms, privacy purposes/retention/vendors and offer wording.
4. **Customer release closeout:** exact environment, signed artifact, store/test
   track, physical Android matrix, auth/location/directions/account acceptance.
5. **Merchant authority path:** implement/verify full or controlled surface with
   authenticated exact-shop capabilities, audit, pause/revoke and no shared admin.
6. **Catalog bootstrap:** canonical reuse, bounded candidate review, useful listing
   depth and truth/freshness states for the chosen cell/domains.
7. **QR path, if selected:** server-authoritative one-winner confirmation, physical
   two-device acceptance, merchant training, reconciliation and pause control.
8. **Lean operations:** one operator/backup, support channel/hours, P0/P1 routes,
   merchant verification/catalog queue and incident drills.
9. **Minimum observability:** environment/release separation, crash/critical health,
   QR/data-quality reconciliation and actionable alerts.
10. **Exact-artifact go/no-go:** freeze roster/message/cohort/artifact, validate every
    P0 gate and authorize only the chosen bounded scope.
11. **Controlled launch:** admit smallest cohort, expand one dimension at a time,
    record continue/hold/narrow/pause decisions.
12. **Commercial learning:** measure merchant/customer continuation and actual unit
    effort before selecting a scaled offer or second district.

## Parallel-safe lanes

After root decisions, field inventory, legal/privacy review, Android release
evidence, controlled merchant-surface implementation, monitoring/runbooks and
catalog preparation can progress in parallel with explicit contracts. QR final
acceptance waits for merchant authority and exact artifacts. Customer acquisition
waits for supply, support and health gates.

## Launch blockers

- no owner-selected mission/cell/cohort/allowlist/operating offer;
- current Merchant App gap with no approved controlled alternative;
- unknown or ambiguous exact-shop authority;
- missing useful catalog density or freshness promise;
- QR selected but physical exact-artifact evidence absent;
- Production/config/migration/RLS/RPC/storage/auth/realtime state not authorized and
  verified by release owners;
- missing legal/privacy/terms/store review for actual scope;
- no staffed support/incident/monitoring/rollback path;
- Android artifact/store gates incomplete, or iOS promised without readiness;
- open P0 or contaminated evidence.

## Non-blockers for the smallest pilot

Ads, economic Reward/Gamification, composite reputation, legacy taxonomy final
migration, district-wide coverage, all 24 L1 depth, enterprise operations tooling,
full multi-shop Merchant App and iOS parity—provided the owner explicitly selects a
bounded Android/controlled merchant path and its safety gates pass.
