# Release Postmortem Model

**State:** PROPOSED — BLAMELESS LEARNING

Postmortems are reserved for meaningful P0/P1 incidents, repeated P2 patterns, serious near misses or owner-requested learning—not every minor defect.

## Record

- concise impact, affected users/systems/versions and duration;
- factual timeline with detection, decision, containment and recovery;
- technical and organizational contributing conditions;
- why existing tests, review, monitoring or gates did not prevent/detect sooner;
- what worked and what delayed response;
- corrective actions with owner, priority, evidence and revisit date;
- new regression, contract, monitoring or release control.

Avoid blame, hindsight certainty, vanity action counts and exposing abuse-detection or personal data. Root cause may be multiple interacting controls rather than one individual mistake.

OWNER_DECISION_REQUIRED: approve postmortem publication/access and follow-up accountability.
