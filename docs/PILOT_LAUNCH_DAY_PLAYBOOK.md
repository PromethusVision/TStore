**State:** `OPERATOR PLAYBOOK — NOT EXECUTED`

## T-24 hours entry gate

- owner-selected scope, promise, platform, cohort and allowlist recorded;
- exact signed artifact manifest/hash/environment verified;
- Production/backend/RLS/RPC/storage/auth/realtime and rollback gates passed by the
  authorized release process;
- selected shops are individually and collectively launch-ready;
- support hours/channels, incident lead, backups and pause controls staffed;
- monitoring/alerts and QR reconciliation drill passed;
- acquisition sources and messages match the actual supply boundary.

Any failed P0 gate means `NO-GO`.

## Launch sequence

| Window | Action | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| `T-60` | Freeze roster/message/artifact; check queues and kill switches | Signed checklist |
| `T-30` | Merchant roll call and listing/QR spot sample | Ready roster/exceptions |
| `T0` | Admit smallest cohort/source | Cohort/source timestamp |
| `T+30` | Check startup/auth/discovery/search/shop/directions and support | Health snapshot |
| `T+60` | If enabled, reconcile first QR outcomes | Authoritative IDs/rejections |
| `T+120` | Review truth, support load and acquisition quality | Go/hold/narrow decision |
| End of day | Close/assign incidents; freeze factual scorecard | Daily evidence pack |

## Launch controls

Expand one dimension at a time: cohort, cell, domain, merchant or channel. Do not
increase paid traffic while supply/health/support is degraded. Operators can pause
customer acquisition or the affected feature without erasing evidence.

## Immediate stop

Cross-environment/artifact uncertainty, cross-shop access, secret/token exposure,
duplicate or wrong-shop verified purchase, unsafe regulated product, misleading
availability at scale, missing incident owner, or unreconcilable state.
