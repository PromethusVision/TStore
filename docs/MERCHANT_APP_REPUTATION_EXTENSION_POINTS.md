# Merchant App Reputation Extension Points

Status: **PROPOSED — NO FINAL GAMIFICATION MODEL**
Wave: 17 / WP48

## Safe inputs

- Verified purchase evidence and eligible review aggregates.
- Shop/catalog freshness and policy compliance.
- Confirmed operational reliability signals with documented semantics.

## Extension boundaries

- Reputation is separate from paid advertising and organic ranking.
- Merchant cannot directly edit score/badge inputs.
- A badge must state criteria, evidence window, expiry/revocation and appeal.
- Low-volume confidence and policy suspensions require safe handling.
- Rewards/gamification cannot incentivize fraudulent QR confirmation or review pressure.

## Proposed interfaces

- Read-only eligibility/result summary for merchant.
- Actionable remediation without exposing anti-abuse thresholds.
- Versioned criteria and immutable award/revoke audit.
- Customer-facing disclosure only after owner/policy approval.

No badge names, weights, thresholds or scoring formula are finalized here.
