# Ecosystem Ads / Reward / Reputation Separation

**Result:** PASS — FOUR INDEPENDENT AUTHORITIES

| System | May consume | May produce | Must never produce |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ads | eligible listing, contextual intent, coarse location, campaign budget | disclosed sponsored render/interaction/billing evidence | organic rank, review right, purchase, reward, reputation badge |
| Reward | approved verified purchase/correction and policy | immutable earn/adjust/reverse/redeem/expire ledger | review right, purchase, rating or ad attribution |
| Gamification | trusted and explicitly approved strong/soft events | explainable badge/progress state | punitive social-credit score or economic promise |
| Merchant reputation | verified operational evidence and independent ratings | explainable signal/projection/badge | hidden rating rewrite or paid trust status |

## Non-negotiable boundaries

- Spend buys only disclosed eligible ad delivery; relevance/locality/organic order
  and trust signals stay independent.
- Viewing/clicking Ads does not earn Reward by default and never creates purchase.
- Verified purchase may independently feed reviews, future Reward and factual
  reputation, but each evaluator has its own idempotency/policy.
- Customer ratings remain visible and cannot be suppressed by proprietary
  reputation.
- Paid verification or subscription may unlock tooling/service, never an organic
  quality badge.

Pilot recommendation: Ads, Reward, gamification and advanced/public reputation are
DEFER. Architecture completeness is not launch necessity.
