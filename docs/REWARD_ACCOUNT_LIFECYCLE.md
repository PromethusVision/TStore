# Reward Account Lifecycle

**State:** PROPOSED — PRIVACY/LEGAL/OWNER DECISIONS REQUIRED

| Event | Economic ledger | Derived state | Identity/privacy |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sign-up | No automatic reward | Empty | Stable customer ID; no public profile assumption. |
| Email/account recovery | Unchanged | Rebuild from same identity | Recovery cannot duplicate account/value. |
| Account merge | Never concatenate balances blindly | Recalculate after duplicate-event/fraud review | Owner-approved merge proof required. |
| Deletion request | Preserve only required economic/audit evidence under reviewed retention/pseudonymization | Remove public badge/progress surfaces | Do not expose purchase history; explain residual obligations. |
| Re-registration | New identity by default | No inherited progress | No email-based silent relinking. |
| Fraud suspension | Freeze unsettled actions | Hide/mark pending where appropriate | Appeal and human review. |

Deletion cannot erase merchant purchase evidence or accounting obligations in an uncontrolled way, yet retention must be purpose-limited. Exact retention/anonymization requires legal/privacy decision. Review evidence and reward ledger remain separate.
