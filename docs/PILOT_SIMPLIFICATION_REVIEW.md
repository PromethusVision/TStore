# EsnaftaVar Esenler Pilot — Simplification Review

**State:** `MINIMUM COHERENT CONFIGURATION — OWNER REVIEW REQUIRED`

## Keep

- one owner-selected Esenler micro-cell;
- `12–25` launch-ready merchants as a planning band, subject to real density;
- `3–5` ordinary, useful product domains;
- guest discovery/search/product/shop/directions with truthful empty states;
- canonical reuse plus bounded assisted listings/candidates;
- exact-shop merchant authority and a controlled minimum merchant surface;
- QR only as a small trained cohort or staged feature;
- Android-first controlled distribution;
- one trained operator, launch backup and named specialist escalation;
- 15 governed KPIs plus low-cost health/data-quality monitoring;
- explicit pause and one-dimension expansion controls.

## Remove from first launch

- district-wide coverage claim;
- mandatory 100-merchant threshold;
- all 24 L1 category completeness;
- full Merchant App as a universal blocker;
- iOS parity as day-one requirement;
- sponsored ads, billing and attribution;
- economic rewards, levels, challenges and streaks;
- composite reputation/badges;
- regulated/high-risk domains without approved evidence;
- enterprise support/CRM/APM/SIEM;
- checkout, payment, shipping and classic orders;
- legacy taxonomy migration.

## Preserve despite simplification

Do not remove exact-shop authorization, audit, QR atomicity/idempotency, catalog
identity/truth, privacy/data minimization, exact-artifact release evidence, support
and incident ownership. These are safety foundations, not optional complexity.

## Simplified first proof

Can a small set of Esenler customers, using one exact Android release, repeatedly
find truthful nearby product/shop options in one cell while a lean team and selected
merchants keep the supply accurate and safely resolve QR/support exceptions?
