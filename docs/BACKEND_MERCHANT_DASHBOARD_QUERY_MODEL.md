# Backend Merchant Dashboard Query Model

**State:** PROPOSED — NO DASHBOARD/API

Dashboard reads are exact-shop or explicitly authorized organization roll-ups.
Every metric declares definition version, authority grade, window/time zone,
freshness, correction posture and privacy threshold.

## Recommended priority

1. action-required listing, QR, review/report and catalog health;
2. distinct server-authoritative verified physical purchases;
3. QR success/failure health with restricted reason detail;
4. active/unavailable/stale listing counts;
5. eligible review count/rating distribution;
6. aggregate product/shop views and directions intent after privacy approval.

Views, search, wishlist, cart, directions and shop opens are **soft intent**, not
sales, visits, revenue or conversion. Verified purchase is also not payment,
invoice or audited revenue. Item quantity/price is not revenue unless a separately
approved monetary-trust contract exists.

Low-count customer breakdowns, UUID/contact, exact journey/location and private
review/chat content are prohibited. Complete local-day and 7/30-day windows are
preferred over misleading partial comparisons. Final metrics and thresholds are
`OWNER_DECISION_REQUIRED`.
