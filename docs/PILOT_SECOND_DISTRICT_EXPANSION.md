# EsnaftaVar Pilot — Second-District Expansion

**State:** `EXPANSION CRITERIA — NO DISTRICT SELECTED`

## Expansion is replication, not a merchant-count milestone

The hypothesis “expand at 100 active merchants” is insufficient. One hundred
merchants may be scattered, stale, policy-heavy or manually unsupportable. A smaller
portfolio may prove a repeatable cell; a larger one may still be unready.

## Required evidence

| Gate | Evidence before expansion |
|---|---|
| Esenler core value | Repeated qualified discovery and honest empty-state behavior |
| Supply | Several stable `USABLE` cell-domain pairs with retained ready shops |
| Catalog | Reusable product identity, bounded candidate queue and truth/freshness control |
| Merchant operations | Onboarding/maintenance/QR can run without hidden owner heroics |
| Customer trust | Promise, location/login and QR meaning understood; critical complaints controlled |
| Reliability | Exact artifacts and critical health within approved operating envelope |
| Support | Case load/cadence and backup sustainable with measured unit effort |
| Economics | Acquisition and operations ranges understood; no financial guarantee |
| Governance | Allowlist, privacy/legal/store and incident controls portable |
| Learning | At least one failed/weak hypothesis handled without corrupting evidence |

## Candidate district screen

Geographic adjacency or operational accessibility, merchant concentration in proven
domains, customer local-travel pattern, ability to reuse catalog, policy complexity,
channel partnerships, support route and a comparison/holdout design. Do not select
by population alone.

## Expansion modes

Duplicate one proven micro-cell; add one adjacent cell; or launch a deliberately
different comparison cell. Expand only one or two dimensions so outcomes remain
explainable.

## Stop

Esenler still depends on exceptional manual intervention, metrics are contaminated,
retention is incentive-only, QR/catalog integrity is unresolved, or second-district
staffing/support cannot be named.
