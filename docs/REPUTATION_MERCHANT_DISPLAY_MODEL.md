# Merchant Reputation Display Model

Status: **PROPOSED — NO UI IMPLEMENTATION**
Wave: 18 / Workstream AG

## Candidate surfaces

- Shop card: at most one concise factual badge/status with accessible label.
- Shop detail: customer rating/count remains prominent; separate reputation explanations.
- Seller comparison: compact badge only when directly relevant and evidence-current.
- Merchant App: private evidence status, remediation, expiry and appeal—not secret thresholds.

## Rules

- Badge never replaces/hides eligible rating or `Sponsorlu` disclosure.
- Verification badge says identity/policy fact, not “recommended/best/quality guaranteed”.
- Insufficient history shows no negative score.
- Shop-specific badge does not automatically apply to sibling branches or organization.
- Expired/revoked badge disappears/changes according to safe lifecycle; historical customer reviews remain.
- Customer can reach a short “Bu ne anlama geliyor?” explanation.

Exact badge density, naming, placement and scope are owner/UX/legal decisions.
