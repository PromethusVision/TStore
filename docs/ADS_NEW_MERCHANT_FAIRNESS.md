# New-Merchant Advertising Fairness

**State:** PROPOSED FAIRNESS MODEL — NO NUMERIC THRESHOLD FINALIZATION

## Problem

A strict historical-review or conversion requirement would permanently privilege
incumbents. Removing all quality checks would expose customers and legitimate
advertisers to fake shops/listings. V1 needs evidence-based entry plus bounded
exploration, not reputation inheritance.

## Proposed entry path

- active authenticated merchant and real active shop;
- shop/listing ownership and physical location evidence;
- exact eligible product listing with fresh price/stock;
- required product/merchant policy checks;
- conservative initial budget/density/geo exposure;
- same disclosure, fraud and truth standards as established merchants;
- no minimum review count if stronger identity/listing evidence passes;
- gradual delivery expansion based on valid traffic and absence of abuse, not paid
  spend alone.

## Fair selection

- reserve bounded exploration opportunity among eligible campaigns;
- rotate when candidates are similarly relevant rather than always favor history;
- merchant-level/page caps prevent large catalogs from dominating;
- do not use sparse/no-review state as proof of poor quality;
- do not guarantee impressions or position to a new merchant;
- established merchant cannot buy exclusion of a qualified newcomer.

## Anti-abuse

Campaign/account cycling does not reset merchant/shop/target history. New account
status does not bypass policy review. High-risk changes or related suspended
identities require proportionate review without exposing detection logic.

## Metrics to review

Eligible-to-served rate, time to first qualified impression, rejection reason,
invalid-traffic rate and complaint rate should be compared by merchant tenure with
minimum sample/privacy rules. A difference is an investigation signal, not proof of
discrimination or abuse.
