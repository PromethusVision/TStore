# Merchant Reputation Dispute Model

Status: **PROPOSED — OWNER/POLICY REVIEW REQUIRED**
Wave: 18 / Workstream AM

## Merchant may dispute

Incorrect merchant/shop identity, expired evidence processing, catalog/availability measurement error, duplicate/attributed abuse event, badge scope, factual policy status or system-calculation defect.

## Merchant may not

- Directly delete/edit/hide a legitimate customer review or rating.
- Buy a correction, badge or organic reputation through ads/rewards.
- Demand customer identity or anti-fraud thresholds.
- Rewrite verified transaction evidence.

## Flow

Submit governed reason/evidence → deduplicate open case → authorized review → uphold/correct/needs-info → append correction/recompute derived state → safe merchant/customer projection → appeal where available.

## Historical integrity

Correction preserves original signal, decision, effective interval and actor. Public unsupported negative labels are avoided while a restricted investigation is pending, subject to safety/policy rules.
