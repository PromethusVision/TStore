# EsnaftaVar Ad Reporting Requirements

**State:** `PROPOSED — ADS OWNER DECISIONS OPEN`

Candidate report fields: campaign/revision/status, schedule, approved target/surface,
qualified/observed impression definitions, opens, shop opens, directions intents,
invalid-event exclusions, attribution candidates, freshness and model/version.

Every number distinguishes measured, qualified, invalid and unavailable. No event
is billable until pricing/evidence/dispute rules are approved. Attribution is
labelled reporting association, not causality. Verified purchase is not revenue.

Merchant/customer identity, precise location, raw query and small cohorts are
excluded. Organic and sponsored performance remain separable; spend cannot alter
organic rank/reputation/review/reward.

Exports require least privilege, date/row limits, audit and formula/version
metadata. Commercial KPIs, attribution window and billing model remain Product
Owner decisions.
