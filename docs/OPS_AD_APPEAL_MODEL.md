# Advertising Appeal Model

**State:** PROPOSED — NO ADS OR BILLING IMPLEMENTATION

## Appealable decisions

Campaign/creative rejection, policy hold, merchant ad-capability restriction, invalid-traffic exclusion, under-delivery/measurement dispute, or campaign suspension. Organic ranking, guaranteed position, and unavailable inventory are not promised appeal outcomes.

## Required submission

Authenticated merchant/authorized staff, campaign/listing IDs, original decision/reason, contested scope, specific claimed error, bounded evidence, and requested review. Never request customer identities, click logs, passwords, tokens, or competitor information from the merchant.

## Review

Resolve current listing/merchant/policy/campaign versions; preserve original decision; assess new evidence and process error; use independent reviewer for material financial/permanent restriction where possible; record `UPHOLD`, `MODIFY`, `OVERTURN`, `REQUEST_EVIDENCE`, or `ESCALATE`.

## Financial boundary

An appeal may trigger a held amount, non-billable classification, credit/refund review, or report correction only under future approved billing rules. Operator cannot fabricate delivery or promise payout. Verified purchase remains independent and does not automatically prove ad causation.

## Communication

Explain affected object, safe reason class, remediation, decision scope/effective time, reporting freshness, and further appeal. Do not reveal detection thresholds, other merchants, customer PII, or attack methods.
