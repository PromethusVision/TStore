# Unified Review and Badge V1 versus Future Scope

**State:** PROPOSED SCOPE — OWNER REVIEW REQUIRED

## MUST for first implementation wave

- preserve frozen product-review rights;
- one visible flow with separate product and structured-shop outputs;
- immutable origin shop/evidence and idempotent section submission;
- three-versus-four question experiment, skip/N/A and question version;
- per-purchase raw response with explicit effective-contribution policy;
- no public merchant aggregate/badge by default;
- privacy notice, moderation/correction and analytics separation.

## SHOULD after evidence

- internal dimension distributions and Model A audit baseline;
- Model B + D offline evaluation, sample/fairness/fraud monitoring;
- merchant-facing private explanation and case process;
- rolling/freshness simulation and primary badge comprehension tests.

## DEFER

- public merchant star, organization roll-up and ranking;
- composite/meta badges and `Mahallenin Yıldızı`;
- exponential decay, sector-specific weights and opaque priors;
- recommendation/search dependence on badges;
- automated punitive fraud score or sentiment-derived merchant score.
