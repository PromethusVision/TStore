# Reward V1 vs Future Scope

**State:** RECOMMENDED — OWNER DECISION REQUIRED

## Contrarian starting point

The first commercial Esenler pilot does not need an economic Reward Engine to validate discovery, merchant-confirmed QR purchases and verified reviews. Funding, accounting, regulated-product incentives, reversals and disputes create disproportionate pre-product-market-fit risk.

## MUST_HAVE before any reward implementation

- Preserve the server-authoritative verified-purchase event and stable identities.
- Preserve review independence: repeat/quantity/reward never creates another review right.
- Decide pilot timing, funding liability, earning basis, scope, unit, redemption, expiry and policy exclusions.
- Specify immutable ledger, idempotency, reversal, audit, appeal and account-deletion semantics.
- Prove unknown policy, duplicate events and client-only evidence issue zero value.

## SHOULD_HAVE before a limited shadow pilot

- Non-economic shadow calculation with no customer promise.
- Merchant/shop scope and new-merchant fairness finalized.
- Explainability copy, support playbooks, anomaly monitoring and kill switch.
- Legal, privacy and accounting reviews completed.

## DEFER

- Economic issuance/redemption, platform-wide transferable value, spend weighting and merchant self-configuration.
- Levels, purchase streaks, spend-dependent challenges and ad-linked earning.
- Production activation until an explicit owner release gate.

**Recommendation:** `POST_PILOT`; shadow mode only after root decisions. Not owner-approved.
