## Cross-cutting rules

- Badge does not imply wealth, quality of person, reward balance or review authority.
- Purchases/shops/products use stable IDs and source event deduplication.
- Directions, wishlist and view are weak signals and cannot support strong public claims alone.
- Policy-sensitive/excluded products do not create incentives without explicit allowlist.
- Badge names, thresholds, public surfaces and lifecycle remain `OWNER_DECISION_REQUIRED`.

## Starting hypothesis

If badges are eventually piloted, test one private verified-local-shopper badge and one sentiment-neutral review-contributor badge. Levels, leaderboards and purchasing streaks are not required.
