# EsnaftaVar Esenler Pilot — Conceptual 30/60/90-Day Phases

**State:** `SEQUENCE OPTIONS — NOT A CALENDAR COMMITMENT`

Days are conceptual review windows. Entry gates, not elapsed time, advance phases.

| Window | Mission | Typical scope | Exit evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–30 | Prove safe usefulness | One cell, ordinary domains, controlled cohort, assisted merchant path | Core tasks understood; no unresolved invariant failure; truth/support manageable |
| 31–60 | Prove repeatability | Adjacent cell or more merchants in same domains, one bounded acquisition experiment | Acquisition and onboarding repeated; retention/queue/unit effort understood |
| 61–90 | Prove operating model | Moderate scope, reduced manual dependency, offer hypothesis review | Sustainable cadence, exact release evidence and go/narrow/pause/expand decision pack |

## Phase rules

- No automatic day-based expansion.
- Repair weeks do not count as failure if learning is explicit and safety holds.
- Add only one major variable at a time.
- Ads, rewards/gamification and public reputation stay outside these phases unless a
  separate owner decision and their full entry gates are satisfied.
- A paid offer or end of free period needs advance notice and separate evidence.
