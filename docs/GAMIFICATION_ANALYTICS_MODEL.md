# Gamification Analytics Model

Status: **PROPOSED — PRIVACY/METRIC REVIEW REQUIRED**
Wave: 18 / Workstream AV

## System-health metrics

- Eligible source events vs evaluated/held/reversed.
- Duplicate/idempotency suppression and correction rate.
- Reward earn/redeem/expire/dispute counts by program with liability context.
- Badge award/revoke/appeal and insufficient-evidence rates.
- New-vs-established merchant badge eligibility fairness.
- Notification opt-out/complaint and dark-pattern trust signals.

## Avoid vanity metrics

App opens, taps, notification opens, badge count and time-in-app do not prove customer value. Purchase volume alone cannot prove quality or responsible reward design.

## Privacy

Aggregate/cohort thresholds, restricted fraud metrics, no individual customer purchase drilldown for merchants, clear freshness and zero/delayed/suppressed distinctions. Ads analytics remains a separate identity/model.

## Evaluation

Success requires trust, low dispute/abuse, understandable rules and incremental local-commerce value—not engagement at all costs.
