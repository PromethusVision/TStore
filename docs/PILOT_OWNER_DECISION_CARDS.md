# EsnaftaVar Esenler Pilot — First 15 Owner Decision Cards

**State:** `RECOMMENDED REVIEW ORDER — NO OPTION SELECTED`

These are the first 15 decisions to review. Recommendations are Agent 3 advice,
not approvals.

## 1 — Pilot mission (`PR-01`)

- **Question:** Is the first proof local discovery usefulness, merchant digitization,
  monetization or all at once?
- **Options:** discovery-first; merchant-first; monetization-first; multi-goal.
- **Recommended:** discovery-first with merchant truth as a non-negotiable guardrail.
- **Why/tradeoff:** clean learning and smaller scope; monetization evidence comes later.

## 2 — Launch cell and density (`PR-02`)

- **Question:** One micro-cell, adjacent cells or district-wide?
- **Options:** one; 2–3; whole district.
- **Recommended:** one usable cell, then an adjacent-cell gate.
- **Why/tradeoff:** coherent choice/support versus narrower reach.

## 3 — Release exposure (`PR-03`)

- **Question:** Invite/closed, bounded public or broad public?
- **Recommended:** invite/closed then bounded public.
- **Tradeoff:** safer evidence with cohort bias.

## 4 — Domain allowlist (`PR-04`)

- **Question:** Which product/merchant domains are promised?
- **Recommended:** a few ordinary low-policy product domains with real density.
- **Tradeoff:** lower policy load, smaller advertised breadth.

## 5 — Merchant offer (`PR-05`)

- **Question:** Free period, milestone participation, free basic, paid validation or
  research followed by a new offer?
- **Recommended:** research/milestone model with an explicit later offer review.
- **Tradeoff:** less sales certainty now, better expectation and willingness-to-pay evidence.

## 6 — Merchant operating path (`PR-06`)

- **Question:** Wait for full Merchant App or use a controlled minimum path?
- **Recommended:** capped controlled path plus bounded assisted bootstrap.
- **Tradeoff:** earlier learning, managed temporary operational debt.

## 7 — Verification evidence (`PR-07`)

- **Question:** Uniform minimal, stronger universal or risk-tiered evidence?
- **Recommended:** risk-tiered, with ordinary-domain minimum.
- **Tradeoff:** proportional friction but greater policy design.

## 8 — Listing freshness (`PR-08`)

- **Question:** What can customers be promised about price/availability?
- **Recommended:** domain-tiered windows with visible `UNKNOWN`.
- **Tradeoff:** honest state at the cost of reminders and governance.

## 9 — QR timing (`PR-09`)

- **Question:** All day one, staged, limited cohort or later?
- **Recommended:** limited trained cohort or staged after discovery acceptance.
- **Tradeoff:** bounded incident load; mixed availability must be clear.

## 10 — Guest/login/location (`PR-10`)

- **Question:** How much value is visible before identity/location?
- **Recommended:** guest discovery, login at protected actions, contextual location
  request and manual area fallback.
- **Tradeoff:** lower friction/privacy cost, more state testing.

## 11 — Platform scope (`PR-11`)

- **Question:** Android-only, Android-first with iOS lane or dual launch?
- **Recommended:** Android-first with explicit iOS readiness lane.
- **Tradeoff:** faster pilot but known reach bias.

## 12 — Customer acquisition (`PR-12`)

- **Question:** Organic/merchant/referral first, bounded Meta test, or broad paid?
- **Recommended:** organic first; paid test only after density/support/measurement gates.
- **Tradeoff:** slower reach, cleaner core signal.

## 13 — Support/staffing (`PR-13`)

- **Question:** Owner-only, one operator hybrid, team or outsourced?
- **Recommended:** one trained operator + owner policy + named specialist escalation
  and launch-window backup.
- **Tradeoff:** lean and learnable, but cohort must stay capped.

## 14 — KPI/monitoring (`PR-14`)

- **Question:** What evidence governs continue/narrow/pause/expand?
- **Recommended:** 15-metric minimum scorecard, environment/release separation,
  provider logs and one privacy-reviewed crash/error tool if approved.
- **Tradeoff:** actionable baseline, not an exhaustive analytics warehouse.

## 15 — Ads/reward/reputation timing (`PR-15`)

- **Question:** Day one, stabilization or post-pilot for each engine?
- **Recommended:** post-pilot for all; retain independent review rating/count.
- **Tradeoff:** postpones monetization/incentive features, protects focus and trust.

## Review instruction

For each card record selected option only in a Product Owner decision session, plus
rationale, effective scope/date, dependent decisions and revisit trigger. This file
contains no selection.
