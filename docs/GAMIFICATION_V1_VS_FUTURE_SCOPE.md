# Customer Gamification V1 vs Future Scope

**State:** RECOMMENDED — NOT OWNER APPROVED

## Pilot recommendation

Ship no public gamification in the first commercial pilot. If later evidence shows value, begin with a small set of private, explainable badges based only on strong server-authoritative events.

| Capability | Recommended timing | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Customer badges | POST_PILOT | Needs evidence, privacy, correction and comprehension gates. |
| Levels | DEFER | Adds social rank and complexity without proven local-commerce value. |
| Challenges | DEFER | Risk of unnecessary-spend incentives; discovery-only design needs testing. |
| Purchase streaks | DO_NOT_RECOMMEND | Encourages pressure, loss aversion and unhealthy purchase cadence. |
| Public customer reputation | EXCLUDE | No social-credit or punitive customer score. |

Before badges: approve families/evidence, lifecycle/revocation, display privacy, stable identity, event idempotency and appeal. Wishlist, views and directions are weak analytics and cannot independently grant durable status.
