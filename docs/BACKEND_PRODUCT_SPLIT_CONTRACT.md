Split corrects one over-broad canonical identity into distinct successors. It is
more ambiguous than merge and therefore fails closed.

## Rules

- preserve predecessor and append child lineage;
- new listings/candidates must select an evidenced child;
- existing listing moves only with reliable product/variant evidence;
- historical purchases/reviews stay on predecessor when child is ambiguous;
- ad/reward/reputation targeting pauses or re-evaluates; it never inherits to the
  first child;
- taxonomy and search aliases route to neutral resolution when ambiguous;
- impact counts, unresolved queue and every mapping remain auditable.

Default-successor assignment is prohibited. Treatment of predecessor reviews and
customer-visible neutral history is `OWNER_DECISION_REQUIRED`.
