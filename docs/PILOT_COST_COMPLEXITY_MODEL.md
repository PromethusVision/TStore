# EsnaftaVar Esenler Pilot — Cost and Complexity Model

**State:** `RELATIVE MODEL — NO BUDGET OR FINANCIAL PROMISE`

## Cost ledger

Track time and cash separately across merchant acquisition, onboarding/verification,
catalog bootstrap/maintenance, customer acquisition, support, policy review,
monitoring/tooling, release/store, incident reserve, legal/privacy/accounting advice
and merchant/customer research. Volunteer/owner time is still cost.

## Relative scenario load

| Cost driver | Very lean | Moderate | Broader |
|---|---|---|---|
| Merchant field/acquisition | `M` | `L` | `XL` |
| Assisted onboarding/catalog | `L` per merchant, bounded total | `XL` without self-service | Unsustainable without tooling |
| Verification/policy | `M` ordinary allowlist | `L` with more domains | `XL` with regulated breadth |
| Customer acquisition | `S–M` invite/organic | `M–L` experiments | `XL` repeatable mix |
| Support/incident coverage | `M` one operator + backup | `L` hybrid | `XL` multi-function |
| Monitoring/release | `M` mandatory baseline | `M–L` | `L–XL` |
| Ads/Reward/Reputation | `NONE` | `NONE/SHADOW` | Separate business case |

`S/M/L/XL` are relative effort, not currency.

## Manual versus paid tooling

| Need | Manual/free-first candidate | Buy/automate when |
|---|---|---|
| Case intake | Structured form + governed queue | Volume, access, audit or SLA exceeds safe process |
| Monitoring | Provider logs + small dashboard + one crash tool | Retention, alerting or correlation gap is demonstrated |
| Merchant onboarding | Checklist and assisted session | Repeated steps consume capacity and authority can be preserved |
| Catalog QA | Sampling and reason-coded queue | Backlog/duplicates justify safe automation |
| Campaigns | No ad engine | Core value and full ads prerequisites are proven |

## Unit-cost hypotheses to observe

Operator minutes per launch-ready merchant, useful listing, candidate resolution,
truth correction, supported customer, QR exception, retained merchant and qualified
customer. Do not divide by vanity signups or count unpaid owner work as zero.

## Complexity stop rule

If a new feature adds a new authority, money ledger, policy queue, 24/7 expectation
or opaque metric before it improves the primary pilot question, defer it.
