# Regulated Product Dossiers

**State:** RESEARCH-BASED FAIL-CLOSED DOSSIERS — SPECIALIST REVIEW REQUIRED

Each dossier separates taxonomy, listing, advertising, reward and merchant gates.
The recommendation is deliberately narrower than a possible future lawful scope so
the Esenler pilot can launch without interpreting specialist regimes ad hoc.

## 1. Alcohol

- **Evidence:** the Ministry's [26 June 2026 notice](https://www.tarimorman.gov.tr/TADAB/Duyuru/280/Alkollu-Icki-Reklamlarina-Iliskin-Mevzuatta-Yapilan-Degisiklik-Hakkinda-Duyuru)
  describes broad advertising/promotion limits under amended Law 4250; the official
  sales/presentation regulation also restricts internet sale/presentation.
- **V1:** sponsored ads, rewards, challenges, promotions and product-driven push are
  `EXCLUDED`. Neutral shop/listing discovery is `LEGAL_REVIEW_REQUIRED`, not assumed.
- **Reviewer:** `LAWYER` with alcohol-domain regulatory expertise.

## 2. Tobacco, nicotine and e-cigarette-adjacent goods

- **Evidence:** [official sales/presentation regulation](https://www.tarimorman.gov.tr/TADAB/Belgeler/Y%C3%B6netmelikler/yonetmelik_27808_07.01.2011.pdf)
  restricts internet announcements, promotion, campaigns and purchase-conditioned gifts.
- **V1:** product listings, ads and rewards are `EXCLUDED` until an exact item
  classification/allowlist is professionally approved. “Electronic” category is no bypass.
- **Reviewer:** `LAWYER` + `DOMAIN_REGULATORY_SPECIALIST`.

## 3. Human medicines

- **Evidence:** the Ministry maintains a separate [medicinal-product promotion
  regulation](https://www.saglik.gov.tr/TR%2C10458/beseri-tibbi-urunlerin-tanitim-faaliyetleri-hakkinda-yonetmelik.html)
  and regulated distribution framework.
- **V1:** `EXCLUDED` from ordinary catalog/listing/ads/rewards. Do not infer pharmacy
  functionality from `Sağlık & Medikal` or merchant sector.
- **Reviewer:** `DOMAIN_REGULATORY_SPECIALIST` + `LAWYER` before any reconsideration.

## 4. Medical devices and professional devices

- **Evidence:** [Tıbbi Cihaz Satış, Reklam ve Tanıtım amendment](https://resmigazete.gov.tr/eskiler/2023/05/20230526-9.htm)
  shows device, sales-centre, advertising and intermediary duties.
- **V1:** `REGULATED`/`LEGAL_REVIEW_REQUIRED` by intended use, device class, seller,
  registration/conformity and claim. Professional/invasive/diagnostic ambiguity fails closed.
- **Reviewer:** TİTCK/medical-device `DOMAIN_REGULATORY_SPECIALIST`.

## 5. Supplements and medical nutrition

- **Evidence:** the Ministry's [supplement information](https://guvenilirgida.tarimorman.gov.tr/Sayfa/Detay/1882)
  distinguishes approved products and age limits; [Health Claims Supervision](https://resmigazete.gov.tr/eskiler/2023/06/20230613-1.htm)
  separately governs claims.
- **V1:** product approval/evidence is not claim/ad/reward permission. `REGULATED`;
  no disease/treatment/cure language and no health-outcome rewards.
- **Reviewer:** food/supplement `DOMAIN_REGULATORY_SPECIALIST` + lawyer for ads.

## 6. Infant formula and infant products

- **Evidence:** official [infant-formula communiqué](https://istanbul.tarimorman.gov.tr/Belgeler/SolMenu/RESMI%20GAZETE2019-2019/TEBLIGLER/BebekFormulleriveDevamFormulleriT.pdf)
  contains strict advertising/promotion provisions.
- **V1:** formula ads, samples, reward/discount mechanics and recommendation placement
  fail closed. Ordinary baby accessories remain separately eligible after safety checks.
- **Reviewer:** infant-food `DOMAIN_REGULATORY_SPECIALIST` + `LAWYER`.

## 7. Prescription/custom optics and contact lenses

- **Evidence:** [Opticianry Law](https://shgmsmdb.saglik.gov.tr/TR-102383/optisyenlik-hakkinda-kanun.html)
  and [Optical Establishments Regulation](https://www.saglik.gov.tr/TR%2C10467/optisyenlik-muesseseleri-hakkinda-yonetmelik.html).
- **V1:** prescription/custom products and contact lenses are `LEGAL_REVIEW_REQUIRED`;
  merchant sector choice or uploaded photo is not authorization. Non-prescription
  accessories need exact non-medical classification.
- **Reviewer:** opticianry `DOMAIN_REGULATORY_SPECIALIST`.

## 8. Veterinary products and live animals

- **Evidence:** [Law 5996](https://www.tarimorman.gov.tr/TRGM/tamtest/Menu/62/Kanun)
  and [Veterinary Health Products](https://www.tarimorman.gov.tr/HAYGEM/Menu/67/Veteriner-Saglik-Urunleri)
  show authorization and channel controls.
- **V1:** live animals `EXCLUDED`; veterinary medicines/health products fail closed.
  Ordinary pet food/accessories do not inherit that restriction, subject to ordinary rules.
- **Reviewer:** veterinary `DOMAIN_REGULATORY_SPECIALIST`.

## 9. Pesticides and plant-protection products

- **Evidence:** 2025 [sales regulation update](https://www.tarimorman.gov.tr/GKGM/Haber/1231/)
  and [official regulation publication](https://sanliurfa.tarimorman.gov.tr/Duyuru/613/Bitki-Koruma-Urunlerinin-Toptan-Ve-Perakende-Satilmasi-Ile-Depolanmasi-Hakkinda-Yonetmelik)
  establish dealer/reçete controls and internet promotion/sale prohibition.
- **V1:** `EXCLUDED` from ordinary listing/ads/rewards; garden taxonomy and merchant
  verification cannot bypass the rule.
- **Reviewer:** plant-protection `DOMAIN_REGULATORY_SPECIALIST`.

## 10. Hazardous chemicals, biocides, batteries and aerosols

- **Evidence:** Ministry product-safety pages and [detergent rules](https://urunkurallari.ticaret.gov.tr/tr/sektorel-rehber/deterjanlar)
  support labeling/composition/safe-market obligations; specific products vary.
- **V1:** `REGULATED` when hazard/biocidal/transport/safe-use signal exists. Require
  exact product class, labeling, warnings, seller/fulfilment and recall evidence.
- **Reviewer:** chemical/product-safety `DOMAIN_REGULATORY_SPECIALIST`.

## 11. Personal protective equipment

- **Evidence:** [official PPE surveillance guidance](https://www.csgb.gov.tr/Media/3qlp1prb/ki%C5%9Fisel-koruyucu-donan%C4%B1mlar%C4%B1n-piyasa-g%C3%B6zetimi-ve-denetimi.pdf)
  supports conformity, CE, instructions and market surveillance.
- **V1:** ordinary apparel is not PPE; protective intended use triggers `REGULATED`
  evidence and claim review. No “certified/professional protection” from merchant text alone.
- **Reviewer:** PPE/product-safety `DOMAIN_REGULATORY_SPECIALIST`.

## 12. Firearms, ammunition, weapon-like and hunting goods

- **Evidence:** [EGM firearms/knives regulation](https://www.egm.gov.tr/kurumlar/egm.gov.tr/IcSite/ozelguvenlik/Mevzuat/silah-2025/ATESLI-SILAHLAR-VE-BICAKLAR-ILE-DIGER-ALETLER-HAKKINDA-YONETMELIK.pdf)
  and [regulated dealer oversight](https://www.egm.gov.tr/ozelguvenlik/denetleme-islemleri).
- **V1:** firearms, ammunition and clearly regulated weapons `EXCLUDED`. Fishing,
  kitchen and ordinary outdoor goods are not automatically excluded; ambiguous
  weapon-like items need exact allowlist and age/seller analysis.
- **Reviewer:** `LAWYER` + weapons-domain specialist.

## 13. Pyrotechnics and explosives

- **V1:** `EXCLUDED`; neither party/celebration taxonomy nor merchant declaration
  authorizes listing, ads or rewards. Reconsideration requires current specialized
  licensing, age, transport, storage and advertising analysis.
- **Reviewer:** `LAWYER` + explosives/pyrotechnics specialist.

## 14. Precious metal, stones and high-value jewellery

- **Evidence:** [Ministry jewellery-commerce framework](https://ticaret.gov.tr/ic-ticaret/kuyum-ticareti)
  supports authorization-certificate and conduct controls.
- **V1:** `REGULATED`; verify exact merchant/capability and define authenticity,
  purity, price, provenance, fraud, report and badge claims. No blanket “verified
  authentic” label.
- **Reviewer:** jewellery-commerce `DOMAIN_REGULATORY_SPECIALIST` + lawyer.

## 15. Automotive chemicals and safety-critical products

- **V1:** ordinary accessory/part may be eligible with fitment/provenance; oils,
  batteries, corrosive/flammable products, tyres, brakes, restraints, helmets and
  other safety claims require exact product-safety/chemical/PPE evidence. Used/renewed
  condition and recall are separate.
- **Reviewer:** automotive/product-safety `DOMAIN_REGULATORY_SPECIALIST`.

## 16. Bundles and cross-domain products

Bundle/kit is not a category or permission shortcut. Evaluate every component and
apply the strictest relevant product, merchant, ad, reward, age, delivery and claim
gate. Missing component identity fails closed for sensitive signals.
