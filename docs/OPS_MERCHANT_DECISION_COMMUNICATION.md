# Merchant Decision Communication

**State:** PROPOSED — NO FINAL COPY OR CHANNEL

## Required content

- authenticated merchant/shop and affected object;
- decision status and exact scope;
- safe stable reason class;
- current policy/rule version or effective date where useful;
- evidence/correction category requested;
- dependent effects on shop/listing/QR/review/ads;
- temporary/recheck/expiry state;
- appeal route and case reference;
- reporting freshness if financial/ads capability later exists.

## Examples of safe clarity

“Listing paused because current availability could not be verified” is preferable to “policy issue.” “Regulated activity evidence expired; affected capability is paused” is preferable to implying the whole business is unlawful.

## Must not disclose

Customer/reporter identity, private review/support content, another merchant's data, fraud thresholds/device/IP details, security exploit, internal notes, legal speculation, operator personal contact, or service-role/database detail.

## Boundaries

Communication cannot promise approval, rank, sales, QR validation, reputation, reward, or ad delivery. Merchant correction is a new assertion/review, not overwrite of evidence. Permanent or broad actions require proportionate explanation and appeal under owner policy.

## Channels

Prefer authenticated in-app case status. Email may notify and link to secure detail; do not include sensitive evidence. WhatsApp/personal messaging is not automatically approved.
