# Badge Explainability

Status: **PROPOSED — OWNER REVIEW REQUIRED**
Wave: 18 / Workstream AH

Every badge definition must answer:

1. **Why earned?** Plain-language criterion and current status.
2. **What evidence?** Strong/medium/weak class, time window and governing rule version—without private details.
3. **What does it not imply?** No guarantee of quality, wealth, approval, price, availability, ranking or future reward unless explicitly true.
4. **Who/what is scoped?** Customer, merchant organization, shop/branch, product/category.
5. **When can it change?** Expiry/revalidation, correction, revoke, retire and appeal.

## Prohibited explanations

- “Trusted/best/favorite” without measurable independent meaning.
- Opaque score or secret paid threshold.
- Exact fraud/security signals that enable bypass.
- Purchase history that reveals sensitive products/merchants.
- Badge explanation that obscures actual customer rating.

## Acceptance

Badge must be understandable without icon/color alone and remain consistent across Customer App, Merchant App, notifications and support.
