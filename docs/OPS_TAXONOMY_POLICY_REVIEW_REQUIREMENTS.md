## View distinction

| Layer | Question |
|---|---|
| Product Taxonomy | What product is this? |
| Merchant Taxonomy | What kind of business is this? |
| Facets | What properties/compatibility does it have? |
| Policy | May this exact product/listing/claim/merchant/channel operate? |

Operators must never infer policy permission from category placement.

## Required view

Stable node ID, display path at revision, owner-final/provisional/lifecycle state, assignable flag, aliases/predecessor/successor, product counts, current policy groups/gates, merchant verification dependencies, unresolved owner decisions, active listing/ads/reward impact, and last decision/policy version.

## Actions

Suggest correction, route policy review, apply owner-final reclassification, place scoped hold, preview policy change impact, and link catalog/merchant cases. Root L1/L2/L3/L4 design, labels, or policy classes are not operator inventions.

## Safety

Proposal-only nodes are clearly marked and unavailable for authoritative assignment. Rename/move preserves stable identity where semantics remain; split/merge requires dedicated operations. Policy relaxation is explicit and prospective/impact-reviewed. Excluded/regulatory unknown remains fail-closed.
