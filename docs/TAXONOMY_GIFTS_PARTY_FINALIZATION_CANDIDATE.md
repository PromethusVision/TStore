## 5. Primary assignment and boundaries

- A coffee machine, toy, book, jewelry item, chocolate or ordinary home product does not move here because it is gifted. Recipient, occasion and personalization remain facets/capabilities.
- `Hatıra & Hediyelik Objeler` is limited to products whose intrinsic function is commemorative/souvenir presentation, such as plaques, magnets and event favors.
- Occasion-specific coordinated/disposable party tableware stays here; general reusable tableware remains Züccaciye & Mutfak.
- Costume-only products stay here; normal clothing and normally wearable shoes stay in Giyim & Moda/Ayakkabı.
- Party box games remain Oyuncak & Hobi. Cake pans and cooking tools remain Züccaciye & Mutfak.
- Fireworks, sparklers, explosive/pyrotechnic products and helium/pressurized-gas cylinders have no candidate leaf.
