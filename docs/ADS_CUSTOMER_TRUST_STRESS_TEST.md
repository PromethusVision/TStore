# Sponsored Advertising Customer Trust Stress Test

**State:** 150 SYNTHETIC TRUST SCENARIOS — NO UI OR RUNTIME

The scenarios test whether a reasonable customer could misunderstand paid position,
distance, recommendation, price or verification. They are design probes, not user
research or legal conclusions.

|---|---:|

## Root trust rules

1. Every paid placement says `Sponsorlu` at first render and throughout its lifecycle.
2. Paid position never means nearest, cheapest, best, recommended or verified.
3. Real distance, price, stock freshness and advertiser identity remain visible.
4. Organic alternatives and their internal order remain accessible.
5. `Neden Sponsorlu?`, hide and report controls are directly reachable.
6. A creative variant that loses disclosure is not eligible to serve.
7. Trust metrics cannot replace real customer research before launch.
