# Merchant App V1 vs Future

Status: **PROPOSED — OWNER REVIEW REQUIRED**
Wave: 17 / WP87

## MUST

The 16 capabilities in `MERCHANT_APP_MINIMAL_V1.md`: identity/shop/auth, onboarding, catalog/listing/price/availability/candidate/barcode, QR security/reconciliation, audit and critical notifications.

## SHOULD

- Actionable dashboard and basic privacy-safe metrics.
- Read-only review inbox/reporting.
- Listing-health filters.
- Safe bulk availability.
- Minimal staff invitations/capability presets if pilot needs cashier/editor separation.
- Scoped stale read cache and draft recovery.

## DEFER

- Public merchant review replies.
- Advanced multi-branch templates/automatic synchronization.
- Bulk price/retire operations.
- Custom analytics/export.
- Listing-media promotion to canonical.
- Booking/reservation/service pricing engine.

## FUTURE ENGINES

Sponsored advertising, reputation/badges/gamification and rewards. Each remains separate from organic truth, review evidence and verified purchase semantics.
