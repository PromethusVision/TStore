# Separation of Duties

**State:** PROPOSED — LEAN PILOT COMPENSATING CONTROLS

## Actions benefiting from separation

- operator role grant/break-glass activation;
- permanent account/merchant restriction;
- regulated merchant approval or policy exception;
- canonical product merge/split with active dependencies;
- verified-purchase correction;
- broad PII/audit export;
- bulk suspension/retirement;
- privacy/security incident notification/legal decision;
- ad financial adjustment when later enabled.

The proposer, approver, and auditor should be distinct where staffing and risk justify it. Self-benefiting decisions are prohibited.

## One-person pilot reality

One person may necessarily handle intake, review, and decision. Compensating controls:

- narrow feature/sector allowlist and capability scope;
- structured reason/evidence/policy version;
- fresh re-authentication;
- immutable before/after audit;
- cooldown for non-urgent irreversible actions;
- automatic notification to Product Owner/security mailbox for R3/R4;
- delayed independent sample review;
- no ability to erase own audit;
- break-glass expiry;
- reversible temporary containment before permanent action.

## Conflict disclosure

Operator records personal/business relationship, prior involvement, original decision authorship, or financial interest and recuses when an alternative exists. Commercial priority, ad spend, or merchant prominence does not change evidence standards.

## Governance

Separation is action/risk based, not role-title theater. Two clicks from two accounts controlled by one person is not independence. Exact pilot two-person requirements remain owner decisions.
