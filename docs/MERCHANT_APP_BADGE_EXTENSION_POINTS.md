# Merchant App Badge Extension Points

Status: **PROPOSED — FUTURE REPUTATION ONLY**
Wave: 17 / WP65

## Candidate evidence families

Verified merchant/shop status, listing freshness, policy compliance, eligible review aggregates and confirmed operational reliability. Candidate families are not badge definitions.

## Trust rules

- Merchant cannot self-assign or pay for a trust badge.
- Paid placement is labeled sponsored and separate from badge/reputation.
- Criteria, evidence window, confidence, expiry/revocation and appeal are versioned.
- Low sample size cannot imply quality certainty.
- Badge change does not rewrite historical reviews or verified transactions.
- Customer App and Merchant App use the same authoritative badge state.
