# Gamification and Reward Merchant App Requirements

**State:** CONCEPTUAL — NO MERCHANT APP IMPLEMENTATION

## Future capabilities

- View verified-purchase-linked reward candidates without changing the purchase record.
- Opt into approved bounded reward templates; see funding, terms, effective version and liability.
- View program issuance/redemption/reversal reconciliation and anomaly holds.
- Understand reputation evidence, scope, sample/freshness and badge meaning.
- Dispute system evidence with attachments/audit trail; never erase a legitimate review.
- Receive restrained fraud/program health alerts and invoke a program pause for new issuance.

## Prohibited capabilities

- Manual award/reputation edit, paid badge purchase, retroactive threshold/expiry reduction.
- Client-side QR confirmation override or server evidence fabrication.
- Positive-review-conditioned reward, customer purchase-history browsing or public identity exposure.
- Service reputation derived from a product QR without a future service-completion contract.
