# EsnaftaVar Esenler Pilot — Customer Acquisition Model

**State:** `CHANNEL EXPERIMENT DESIGN — NO CAMPAIGN OR CONTACT`

## Acquisition objective

Bring eligible people into a launch cell where supply is already useful, then
measure completion of a real discovery task. Installs, impressions and link clicks
are channel diagnostics, not pilot success.

## Sequence

1. internal exact-artifact acceptance;
2. invite-only/closed-track customers tied to ready merchants/cells;
3. merchant-owned and local-community organic channels;
4. small source-coded paid experiment only after funnel and support are stable;
5. broader visibility only after density, truth, health and retention gates.

Google Play supports internal, closed and open testing; Google recommends internal
testing followed by a small closed group before wider testing. Test-track and
account eligibility must be verified in the real Play Console before use:
[Google Play Console Help](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9845334).

## Channel portfolio

| Channel | Pilot use | Measurement | Guardrail |
|---|---|---|---|
| Merchant in-store card/QR | Reach people at a real participating shop | Source-coded qualified first use | Do not confuse invite QR with purchase QR |
| Merchant social channel | Local trusted audience | Source tag and completed discovery | Merchant consent; no guaranteed benefit |
| Customer referral | Test useful word of mouth | Invited/activated/qualified repeat | No economic reward in first pilot |
| Local community/association | Explain bounded public benefit | Cohort task completion/feedback | No implied institutional endorsement |
| Field demo | Observe first-use comprehension | Moderated task evidence | Voluntary, privacy-minimized, no dark pattern |
| Meta/local paid test | Test incremental local reach | Qualified first use, not clicks alone | Verify current geo controls; hard budget and stop rules |
| App-store organic | Later discoverability | Store view→install→qualified use | Exact listing/artifact; avoid broad promise |

## Meta/local advertising principle

Meta is neither required nor sufficient. If an owner later authorizes spend, start
with one cell, one clear discovery promise, controlled destination, source tagging,
no sensitive audience construction, hard cap and predeclared stop criteria. Current
platform settings, beneficiary/payor, targeting behavior, privacy notices and
commercial-advertising compliance must be reviewed at execution time. No exact
conversion or cost assumption is made here.

## Qualified first use

A suggested measurement event is a real task sequence such as: open supported
artifact → view/search a product/domain in the launch cell → open a valid product or
shop → take a useful next step (seller compare, directions/contact) without a
critical error. Login, cart and QR are optional later stages, not forced acquisition
success.

## Stop rules

- customer traffic enters `THIN`, `STALE` or wrong-area supply;
- source/consent cannot be established;
- install volume rises while qualified first use stays weak;
- support or incident load exceeds capacity;
- paid channel cannot distinguish invalid/accidental traffic;
- commercial message overstates stock, sales, coverage or endorsement.
