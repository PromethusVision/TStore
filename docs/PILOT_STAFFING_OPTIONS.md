# EsnaftaVar Esenler Pilot — Staffing Options

**State:** `OPTIONS — NO HIRING/OUTSOURCING DECISION`

## Minimum functions, not necessarily people

Pilot lead/go-no-go, merchant acquisition/onboarding, merchant verification,
catalog/policy review, customer/merchant support, incident/release lead, monitoring
and privacy/legal/security escalation. One person may combine ordinary functions;
critical authority and evidence remain separated.

| Model | Capacity fit | Strength | Failure mode | Suitable scope |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Owner-operated only | Tiny, short observation | Direct learning/lowest cash | Bottleneck, no independent review, burnout | Pre-pilot only |
| One trained operator + owner | Very lean controlled cell | Clear daily queue, affordable | Single point and interruptions | `12–25` shops with narrow allowlist |
| Hybrid lean + scheduled backup | Moderate cells | Continuity and role coverage | Coordination/training cost | `30–60` shops after evidence |
| Outsourced routine intake | Variable later load | Channel coverage | Privacy/access/quality/vendor lock-in | Only after standard cases and controls |
| Dedicated multi-function team | Broad rollout | Parallel work and resilience | Fixed cost before product proof | Wider public scope |

## Recommended candidate

For a controlled first cell: one trained operator handles ordinary merchant/customer
queues and catalog assistance; Product Owner retains scope/commercial decisions;
named, limited escalation covers release/security/privacy/legal/policy. Schedule
backup for launch windows. This is a recommendation, not a hiring decision.

## Capacity triggers

Add backup, cap growth or narrow scope when open-case age, P0/P1 coverage gaps,
merchant onboarding time, catalog candidate age, QR incidents or unplanned
interruptions exceed the approved operating window. Exact thresholds require a
baseline.
