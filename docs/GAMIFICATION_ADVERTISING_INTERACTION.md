## Independent systems

Advertising controls eligible disclosed distribution; reward controls economic entitlement; customer gamification controls optional achievements; reputation controls factual/bounded trust evidence.

## Rules

- No V1 reward/badge/progress for impression, click or ad watch.
- Ad spend/history never improves merchant reputation/badge, customer review weight or organic status.
- A sponsored journey's later verified purchase is evaluated identically to organic purchase.
- `Sponsorlu` disclosure remains visible beside any independent badge/reward surface.
- Ads can consume bounded reputation/policy eligibility as safety gate, but cannot pay around it.
- Reward/gamification opt-out does not remove organic access; ad-control choice does not reduce earned reward.

Any incentivized-ad mechanic requires a separate high-risk owner/policy/privacy/economic decision and is prohibited until then.
