# Streak Risk Review

Status: **CONTRARIAN REVIEW — PURCHASE STREAKS NOT RECOMMENDED**
Wave: 18 / Workstream S

## Risks

- Encourages unnecessary or accelerated spending to avoid loss.
- Uses loss aversion and anxiety against inactivity.
- Disadvantages customers with limited budgets, mobility or local access.
- Creates split-purchase, multi-account and merchant collusion incentives.
- Daily/weekly deadlines are incompatible with ordinary local purchase cadence.
- Purchase history becomes socially sensitive if streak is public.

## Alternatives

- Non-expiring personal milestones without consecutive-day requirement.
- Private “first local discovery” or educational completion markers.
- Period summaries with no penalty/reset language.
- Merchant catalog freshness reminders as operations, not customer purchase streaks.

## Recommendation

Do not use purchase, review or ad-engagement streaks. Any future low-stakes learning streak needs separate dark-pattern/accessibility research and cannot affect reward value, service or reputation.
