# EsnaftaVar Esenler Pilot — Merchant Acquisition Model

**State:** `CHANNEL AND FUNNEL OPTIONS — NO OUTREACH PERFORMED`

## Objective

Acquire a small, geographically coherent set of merchants who can maintain useful
listing truth and operate the selected verifier path. The goal is not maximum
registrations.

## Target profile

- physical shop inside an owner-selected launch cell;
- ordinary allowed product domain with customer discovery value;
- identifiable shop operator and stable contact channel;
- willingness to maintain a bounded initial catalog;
- ability to use the supported device/release path;
- agreement to honest availability/freshness and QR training where enabled;
- no unresolved policy, identity or shop-authority issue.

## Channel options

| Channel | Strength | Risk/control | Best role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Structured street-by-street field acquisition | High geographic control and direct learning | Time intensive; consistent script/evidence required | Seed one micro-zone |
| Existing merchant/customer referrals | Trust and lower explanation cost | Network bias and duplicate category coverage | Add adjacent high-fit shops |
| Local associations/chambers | Efficient group access | Implied endorsement must not be claimed | Information session after scope is clear |
| Merchant-owned social/WhatsApp channels | Reaches real local customers | Consent, commercial-message and measurement boundaries | Invite customers after shop is ready |
| Inbound landing/waitlist | Low handling cost | Creates unusable scattered demand | Capture future zones; do not auto-activate |
| Paid merchant lead generation | Scalable later | Low-intent leads, consent and cost uncertainty | Post-baseline experiment only |

No channel was contacted or activated by this work.

## Funnel

`identified → contacted → interested → eligibility checked → identity/shop verified
→ catalog prepared → verifier trained → acceptance passed → launch-ready → active
→ retained/paused/exited`

Track transitions by source and launch cell. A merchant is not acquired merely
because contact details or signup exist.

## Prioritization without hidden scoring

Review, but do not silently collapse, these dimensions:

1. cell/domain density gap;
2. customer task value;
3. policy/verification complexity;
4. catalog reuse versus candidate burden;
5. operator/device readiness;
6. truth-maintenance willingness;
7. support time and travel cost;
8. single-merchant concentration risk.

## Offer hypotheses

The pilot may be free, milestone-based or use a future paid hypothesis; none is
selected. Avoid guaranteed customers, revenue, ranking or category exclusivity.
Any “free for three months” offer needs an explicit end state, normal-price notice,
exit/export expectations and willingness-to-pay learning plan.

## Channel kill rules

Pause a channel when it produces repeated wrong-area, low-authority, policy-blocked
or support-heavy leads; when consent/source cannot be proven; or when acquisition
outpaces catalog/verification capacity.
