## Commercialization modes

| Mode | What is launched | Upside | Cost/risk | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Organic + future subscription hypothesis | Discovery, truthful listings, QR/reviews; no ads/rewards | Cleanest test of core value and merchant willingness | Monetization unproven; requires explicit future subscription decision | Preferred first-pilot baseline, not owner-selected |
| Ads-enabled | Adds sponsored listing delivery/reporting/billing | Potential future revenue and merchant visibility | Disclosure, policy, fraud, billing, attribution and support load | Delay until organic value and ad prerequisites are proven |
| Reward/Gamification-enabled | Adds ledger/progress/incentives | May influence repeat behavior | Liability, accounting, fraud, dark-pattern and subsidy distortion | Defer; do not use to mask weak product value |

## Three-month-free hypothesis

“Three months free” is not assumed optimal. Compare before any commitment:

- time-boxed free access with explicit end state;
- outcome/milestone-based pilot participation;
- permanently free basic listing with paid post-pilot tools;
- a short paid validation for selected merchants;
- no commercial charge during controlled research, followed by a new offer.

The decision needs merchant interviews, actual onboarding/support cost, value
evidence and clear notice. A long free period can inflate signups while postponing
willingness-to-pay learning; charging too early can suppress learning before value
exists.
