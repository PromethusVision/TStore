# Sponsored Advertising Review Model

**State:** PROPOSED — ADS RUNTIME/OWNER DECISIONS ABSENT

## Review object

The ads foundation proposes exact active `SHOP_LISTING` as V1 sponsored object, Search-only narrow pilot, persistent textual `Sponsorlu`, independent organic ranking, contextual/coarse-location targeting, and non-auction shadow/flat promotion. None is owner-final.

## Review gates

1. active merchant/shop and required verification;
2. exact listing ownership, existence, price/stock/availability freshness;
3. product/merchant policy allowlist and evidence;
4. campaign lifecycle/version/schedule/budget;
5. targeting relevance/locality and privacy basis;
6. native creative facts/claims/media;
7. disclosure render contract;
8. fraud/invalid-traffic or suspension holds.

## Decisions

`APPROVE_SCOPED`, `REQUEST_CORRECTION`, `POLICY_REVIEW`, `PAUSE`, `REJECT`, `SUSPEND_MERCHANT_AD_CAPABILITY`. Campaign approval never guarantees serving, rank, impressions, clicks, visits, or outcome. Serve-time gates re-evaluate current state.

## Boundaries

Ad spend cannot buy policy approval, merchant verification, reputation, reviews, rewards, organic rank, or stale inventory visibility. Taxonomy/sector membership is context, not permission. Missing sensitive evidence fails closed. Review reasons protect fraud thresholds and reporter identity.

## Dependency failure

If ads remain unimplemented, the queue stays disabled. Operations docs do not authorize billing, payments, Production configuration, or campaign UI.
