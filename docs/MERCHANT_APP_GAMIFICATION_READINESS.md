# Merchant App Gamification Readiness

Status: **PROPOSED BOUNDARIES — NO FINAL MODEL**
Wave: 17 / WP117

## Potential future purpose

Help merchants complete beneficial operational actions such as profile/catalog freshness, training and policy remediation. It must not reward manipulation of customers, reviews, QR confirmations or availability.

## Preconditions

- Owner-approved goals and non-gameable evidence.
- Separation from paid advertising, badges/reputation and reward value.
- Anti-abuse, accessibility and technically inexperienced merchant research.
- Transparent progress, versioned criteria and safe opt-out.
- No dark patterns, artificial urgency or penalty for low customer traffic.

## Prohibited shortcuts

- Points for raw QR scan/confirmation volume without fraud safeguards.
- Incentives for review pressure, mass listing, misleading stock or frequent price churn.
- Gamification score used as merchant authorization/policy approval.
- Customer-visible quality badge derived directly from engagement.

No points, levels, streaks, leaderboard or launch placement is finalized.
