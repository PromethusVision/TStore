# New Merchant Reputation Fairness

Status: **PROPOSED — OWNER REVIEW REQUIRED**
Wave: 18 / Workstream W

## Problem

History-heavy reputation permanently advantages incumbents. Treating missing data as perfect trust exposes customers. The correct state is explicit uncertainty plus factual current eligibility.

## Proposed entry

- Show current verified merchant/shop facts if legitimately earned.
- Label rating as “Henüz yeterli değerlendirme yok” rather than low/zero quality.
- Do not require minimum review/purchase count for ordinary organic eligibility if stronger identity/listing/policy facts pass.
- Give bounded, non-paid opportunity in discovery under independent organic rules.
- Use the same fraud/truth standards as established merchants.

## Anti-gaming

Account/shop cycling does not reset linked upheld abuse or policy history. At the same time, similarity signals alone cannot publicly accuse or permanently block a new legitimate merchant.

## Badge behavior

Low sample prevents quality/popularity badges; it does not show a negative badge. Factual verification and catalog completeness may be independently available.

Exact sufficient-history thresholds, exploration mechanism and appeal remain owner/policy decisions.
