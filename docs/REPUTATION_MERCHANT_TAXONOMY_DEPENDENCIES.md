## Boundary

Merchant sector answers what kind of business it is. It does not prove merchant identity, product authority, service quality, reputation or badge eligibility.

## Permitted uses

- Select applicable verification/policy requirements.
- Choose fair comparison context only after owner approval.
- Explain why certain evidence/status may differ by operating model.
- Support sector-level fairness monitoring with sufficient samples.

## Prohibited uses

- High/low reputation by sector name.
- Direct badge/role/product authorization.
- Comparing regulated/service/retail metrics without semantic normalization.
- Penalizing proposed/unknown sector as bad merchant.
