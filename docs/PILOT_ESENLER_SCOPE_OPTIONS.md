# EsnaftaVar Esenler Pilot — Scope Options

**State:** `OPTIONS — NONE SELECTED`

The official Esenler Municipality climate plan lists 17 neighbourhoods and highly
uneven neighbourhood size using 2021 data. That evidence supports micro-zone
planning, but does not choose a launch area or prove merchant density. Current
field inventory must replace desk assumptions before launch.

Source: [Esenler Belediyesi — İklim Değişikliği Eylem Planı, Table 4](https://esenler.bel.tr/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/IKLIM-DEGISIKLIGI-EYLEM-PLANI.pdf).

## Geographic containment model

A launch cell is a contiguous walkable/short-trip commercial area defined by:

- an explicit boundary and named neighbourhood/streets;
- a verified merchant and listing inventory;
- expected customer travel behavior, not a circular radius alone;
- honest “within pilot area” copy rather than district-wide completeness;
- an operator route that can be serviced during declared hours;
- a control cell or later expansion cell where feasible.

## Three launch-size options

| Dimension | Very lean controlled cell | Moderate multi-cell pilot | Broader Esenler rollout |
|---|---|---|---|
| Working shop band | `12–25` | `30–60` | `75–120` |
| Geography | One contiguous micro-zone | Two or three adjacent cells | Many neighbourhoods/district-wide promise |
| Catalog expectation | Deep in `3–5` ordinary domains | Balanced in `6–10` domains | Broad domain coverage with local gaps labelled |
| Listing workload | Assisted and sampled | Hybrid assisted/self-service | Self-service plus scaled review required |
| Customer release | Invite/closed cohort | Bounded public or expanded closed | Public discovery campaign |
| QR | Small trained cohort | Standardized training and daily reconciliation | Mature tooling, monitoring and merchant support |
| Support | One operator plus named escalations | Scheduled backup needed | Multi-function coverage and queue ownership |
| Acquisition | Merchant referrals, field and local community | Mixed organic plus small paid experiments | Repeatable channel economics required |
| Risk | Sparse choice/selection bias | Coordination and consistency | Truth decay, support overload and wasted acquisition |
| Best use | Prove workflow and trust | Prove repeatability | Prove operating system and wider value |

These counts are assumptions, not sufficiency criteria. A smaller coherent cell can
be more launch-ready than 100 scattered, inactive or thin-catalog merchants.

## Recommended review sequence

Agent recommendation, not a selected option:

1. complete field inventory without publishing;
2. start with a very lean controlled cell and ordinary-product allowlist;
3. expand to a moderate adjacent-cell pilot only after truth, support and QR gates;
4. consider broader Esenler visibility only after repeatable acquisition and
   retention evidence.

## Stop conditions for geographic expansion

- promised category has fewer than two or three genuinely useful sellers;
- median/percentile freshness or accuracy exceeds the approved tolerance;
- operator cannot visit/verify the cell within the service rhythm;
- unresolved P0/P1 incidents exceed capacity;
- customer travel tests show the cell is not meaningfully local;
- demand is being purchased into an empty or misleading area.
