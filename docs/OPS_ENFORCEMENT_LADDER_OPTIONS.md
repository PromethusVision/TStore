# Enforcement Ladder Options

**State:** OPTIONS — OWNER/POLICY DECISION REQUIRED

| Level | Candidate action | Conditions |
|---|---|---|
| EDUCATION/CORRECTION | explain rule, request correction/evidence | low harm, credible mistake, reversible |
| WARNING | formal notice and monitored remediation | substantiated recurrence or material issue |
| TEMPORARY_RESTRICTION | listing/capability/shop/account scope and review time | ongoing risk requiring containment |
| SUSPENSION | broader temporary stop with appeal | serious/repeated or security/policy risk |
| PERMANENT_RESTRICTION | permanent scoped/whole access loss | high-confidence severe/repeated conduct after owner policy |
| INCIDENT_KILL_SWITCH | system/cohort feature disable | systemic harm, not punishment |

## Principles

Proportionality, narrowest effective scope, evidence confidence, harm, recurrence, remediation, account-compromise possibility, vulnerable users, reversibility, policy version, and appeal. Not every case must climb sequentially: credible severe active harm can start with containment. Conversely, report volume alone does not accelerate punishment.

## Decision controls

Reason/evidence, impact preview, effective/review time, communication, appeal, second-review candidate for permanent/broad action, and audit. Restoration/supersession preserves history.

## Open owner decisions

Which violations map to levels; warning expiry/recurrence; temporary durations; permanent restriction authority; cross-account linkage threshold; effect on shops/listings/QR/reviews/ads/rewards; and minimum appeal independence.
