# Operations Moderation Consistency

State: PROPOSED FOR OWNER REVIEW

## Goal

Consistency means materially similar facts under the same policy version produce comparable outcomes. It does not mean identical sanctions when severity, history, jurisdiction, evidence confidence or remediation differs.

## Consistency contract

Every moderation decision should bind:

- canonical case and subject identifiers;
- structured allegation and finding codes;
- evidence references and confidence;
- policy identifier plus effective version;
- aggravating, mitigating and uncertainty factors;
- selected action and public/internal reason codes;
- appeal eligibility and any prior linked decisions.

## Calibration process

1. Select de-identified examples across listings, reviews, media, merchants, QR, ads and policy-sensitive products.
2. Have reviewers decide independently from the same evidence packet.
3. Compare finding, policy applicability, action tier and reason—not merely final labels.
4. Resolve divergence through evidence clarification, policy interpretation or explicit escalation.
5. Record the calibration outcome as guidance linked to a policy version; do not retroactively rewrite cases.

## Allowed variance

Variation is explainable when supported by evidence quality, current risk, repeat behavior, remediation, subject scope or policy version. Variation based on operator preference, merchant value, complaint volume alone, protected characteristics or queue pressure is invalid.

## Guardrails

- No enforcement quotas or target rejection rates.
- Historical examples are guidance, not hidden policy that overrides current rules.
- Repeat-abuse signals prompt review; they do not create an automatic punitive score.
- Ambiguous or regulated cases fail closed into policy review rather than improvised approval.
- A moderator cannot change policy status; policy reviewers maintain versioned interpretations.
- False-positive and appeal outcomes must feed calibration without exposing abuse-detection logic.

## Measurement

Measure agreement separately for finding, severity, policy applicability, action and explanation. Monitor unexplained variance, overturned decisions, repeated reason corrections and cross-queue transfers. Small samples are directional only; no individual performance conclusions should be drawn from them.

## Open owner decisions

- Calibration frequency and sample size by risk tier.
- Which actions require independent second review.
- Acceptable disagreement thresholds before policy clarification is mandatory.
- Whether external quality review is ever justified for regulated domains.
