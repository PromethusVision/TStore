# Merchant Reputation Product Contract

Status: **PROPOSED — OWNER REVIEW REQUIRED; NO RUNTIME**
Wave: 18 / Workstream T

## Definition

Merchant reputation is a versioned, explainable system assessment of limited trust/operational evidence for a merchant organization or shop. It is not customer rating, paid advertising, merchant verification, reward program participation or a universal quality score.

## Separation

| Concept | Meaning | Must remain independent |
|---|---|---|
| Customer rating | Visible customer-generated feedback aggregate | Reputation cannot hide/replace it |
| Verification | Identity/policy fact | Does not prove quality/popularity |
| Reputation | Broader bounded evidence assessment | Cannot be purchased through ads |
| Badge | Explainable marker for a specific criterion | Not an opaque total score |
| Advertising | Paid eligible distribution | Spend/history cannot improve reputation |

## Principles

- Strong and current evidence; no single opaque score by default.
- New merchant is `insufficient history`, never automatically bad.
- Severe verified abuse/policy status may block/suspend independently.
- Customer-visible badges explain criteria, evidence window and non-implications.
- Merchant can dispute evidence through governed process, not erase reviews.
- Shop/organization scope and time decay are owner decisions.

## Recommendation

Prefer a few factual badges/statuses over a composite merchant reputation score. Pilot should rely on visible customer rating plus factual verification/status; broader reputation waits for sufficient verified data.
