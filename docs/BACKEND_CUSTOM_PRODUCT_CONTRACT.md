# Backend Custom Product Contract

**State:** PROPOSED

Custom, handmade, local or unbranded goods are valid catalog subjects without
inventing a brand or barcode. “Custom” does not mean ungoverned.

## Contract

- candidate starts merchant-scoped with provenance and review state;
- canonicalization requires sufficient identity, taxonomy and policy evidence;
- variants/options use governed dimensions where applicable;
- listing owns merchant description, price and availability;
- no fake GTIN, generic “Diğer” canonical product or name-only deduplication;
- service labor remains outside product identity unless a future service catalog
  is explicitly approved.

Cross-merchant deduplication must not merge genuinely unique handmade items.
Recommendation: allow merchant-private candidates, promote only after governed
review. Promotion thresholds remain `OWNER_DECISION_REQUIRED`.
