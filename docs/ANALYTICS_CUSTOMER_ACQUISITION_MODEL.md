# EsnaftaVar Customer Acquisition Metric Model

**State:** `OPTIONS — EXTERNAL ATTRIBUTION NOT IMPLEMENTED`

Candidate stages: campaign platform-reported exposure/click, install/open, first
usable discovery, signup/login if chosen, first directions intent and first
authoritative verified purchase. Each is independently defined.

Meta or another network's self-reported data remains an external source and is not
silently joined to customer identity. Attribution options range from aggregate
campaign reporting, explicit install/referral correlation, approved short
pseudonymous windows to controlled experiments. Cross-app/device tracking is not
assumed.

Cost per acquired customer requires approved acquisition definition, complete
spend data, currency/tax handling and deduplication. A click or install is not an
acquired local-commerce customer by itself. Consent, platform policy and legal
review precede implementation.
