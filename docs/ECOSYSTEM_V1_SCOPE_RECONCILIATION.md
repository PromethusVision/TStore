## Pilot MUST_HAVE

- exact Customer release candidate and environment contract;
- owner-approved Android/iOS distribution scope;
- Product Taxonomy V1 and correct demo/current catalog read behavior;
- secure shop identity and merchant/verifier path for exact-shop QR confirmation;
- current server-authoritative QR/verified purchase/review invariants;
- privacy-safe minimum monitoring, support owner, incident path and data cleanup;
- layered QA including signed artifact and physical two-device QR where required;
- explicit Production go/no-go with current-state inventory and no hidden writes.

## SHOULD_HAVE

- limited shop/listing maintenance for pilot merchants;
- governed merchant identity/onboarding and ordinary-product allowlist;
- listing freshness/unknown semantics;
- minimum product candidate escalation handled manually;
- in-app critical notifications and a small truthful pilot scorecard.

## DEFER

Full multi-branch/staff UX, universal variants, variable-measure transaction flow,
full Merchant Taxonomy runtime, advanced search/facets, Ads, billing/auction,
Reward, levels/challenges, public reputation, enterprise Ops automation, broad
Realtime/event outbox, behavioral personalization and exhaustive CI permutations.
