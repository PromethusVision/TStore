# EsnaftaVar Esenler Pilot — Feedback and Learning Model

**State:** `RESEARCH PLAN — NO PARTICIPANT CONTACT`

## Learning questions

### Customer

- Could the customer find a useful nearby product/shop?
- Was area coverage, availability freshness and QR meaning understood?
- What stopped the next useful step?
- Did registration/location requests feel proportionate?
- Would the customer use discovery again without an incentive?

### Merchant

- Could the shop become ready and keep listings truthful?
- Which tasks required assistance and how long did they take?
- Did customer intent and QR evidence have understandable value?
- Which support burdens or commercial uncertainties block continuation?
- Would the merchant continue under each explicit future offer option?

## Methods

Observed task, short voluntary intercept, structured merchant check-in, support-case
coding, exit/pause interview and aggregate behavioral evidence. Use minimum data,
clear purpose and approved retention/access. Silence is not satisfaction.

## Decision record

Each learning item records hypothesis, source/cohort, observation, confidence,
counterevidence, privacy limit, affected scope, reversible next test and owner
decision if any. Do not promote anecdotes or synthetic scenarios into facts.

## Feedback triage

`SAFETY/P0`, `TRUTH`, `CORE_TASK`, `OPERATIONS`, `COMMERCIAL`, `ACCESSIBILITY`,
`NICE_TO_HAVE`, `OUT_OF_SCOPE`. Safety and truth override growth ideas.
