# Sponsored Advertising — Merchant Sector Eligibility Matrix

**State:** PROPOSED FOR PRODUCT OWNER AND POLICY REVIEW
**Scope:** Design-only risk triage over the 67 proposed assignable merchant-sector leaves. No merchant taxonomy finalization, runtime allowlist, or legal conclusion.

## 1. Interpretation

Merchant Taxonomy answers “what kind of local business is this?” It does not prove “may this exact listing be advertised?” The proposed V1 sponsored object remains an exact `SHOP_LISTING`; current product-domain, merchant, shop, evidence, policy, stock/availability, price, location, creative, budget, and disclosure checks all remain mandatory.

Postures:

- `NORMAL_CANDIDATE`: sector can enter standard merchant review, but exact products/services can still be restricted.
- `REVIEW_REQUIRED`: extra verification, evidence, or operational rules are expected.
- `RESTRICTED_CANDIDATE`: no serving until a sector/product-specific allowlist and policy review exists.
- `EXCLUDED_CANDIDATE`: proposed sector-level exclusion. None is assigned at this broad stage, although exact goods/services can be excluded.

Except for the three owner-final beauty leaves, the 67-leaf hierarchy remains `PROVISIONAL_PROPOSAL`. This matrix cannot finalize it.

## 4. Required Intersection with Product Eligibility

A merchant-sector match is only a weak contextual and review-routing signal. Examples:

- `Pet Shop` does not authorize veterinary medicine or live-animal ads.
- `Aktar` does not authorize supplements, treatment claims, or restricted ingestibles.
- `Optik Mağazası` does not authorize prescription/custom optics or contact-lens ads without the required contract.
- `Medikal Ürün Mağazası` does not authorize prescription/restricted or professional medical products.
- `Bahçe & Yetiştirme Ürünleri Mağazası` does not authorize pesticides/plant-protection products.
- `Kuyumcu` does not by itself prove authenticity, hallmark, value, or lawful provenance.
- `Balıkçılık & Av Malzemeleri Mağazası` does not authorize weapons-like, age-restricted, or regulated items.
- a service-sector assignment does not make a service a valid `SHOP_LISTING` product ad.

Conversely, a multi-sector merchant may advertise an eligible product outside its primary sector only when the exact catalog/listing and merchant authorization independently pass. The engine must not infer inventory from sector membership.

## 5. Service and Mixed-Mode Boundary

The proposed merchant model contains retail, service, and mixed operating modes. V1 listing sponsorship should support only an object whose identity, availability, price/claim contract, and customer destination are well defined. Booking, reservation, service quotation, and service-area advertising require a later object/measurement contract.

Therefore:

- service merchants are not automatically excluded;
- a service-sector campaign is held until a supported sponsored-object type exists;
- product ads from a mixed merchant still require an exact eligible listing;
- offline service outcomes must not become automatic CPA billing;
- operating mode must be read from authoritative merchant state, not inferred from sector name.

## 6. Policy-Sensitive Cohorts

### Food and daily consumption

Fresh/perishable handling, registrations, supplements, age restrictions, health claims, and delivery feasibility require product-specific checks. A shop format does not establish compliance.

### Beauty, infant, and personal services

Claims, ingredients, hygiene, professional authorization, child/vulnerable-user protection, booking availability, and service-price truth need separate rules. Owner-final beauty leaf names do not finalize advertising eligibility.

### Construction, vehicle, and repair

Electrical/gas safety, hazardous chemicals, fitment, installation, road safety, professional work, warranty, and liability-sensitive claims require evidence. Vehicle or device compatibility is a facet/catalog fact, not a sector inference.

### Optics, jewelry, medical, pet, and gardening

These cohorts include regulated, health-related, high-value, welfare-sensitive, or controlled products. Fail closed until exact allowlists, verification ownership, document renewal, moderation, and dispute paths exist.

## 7. Merchant State Prerequisites

Before any sector can produce an eligible candidate:

1. merchant and shop are active;
2. selected sector assignment is current and within owner-approved limits;
3. required identity/business/sector verification is valid;
4. exact sponsored object exists and belongs to the shop;
5. product-domain eligibility passes independently;
6. no suspension, policy hold, unresolved fraud, or stale critical data exists;
7. campaign creative and claims match the exact listing;
8. budget, schedule, targeting, location, and disclosure contracts pass.

Unknown status is not approval.

## 8. Open Owner Decisions

1. Finalize or revise the 14 proposed families and 64 non-beauty assignable leaves.
2. Decide which sector postures launch as allowed, reviewed, restricted, or excluded.
3. Define the verification owner, accepted evidence, expiry, and appeal path for each sensitive cohort.
4. Decide whether V1 supports any service sponsored object or only product listings.
5. Define multi-sector eligibility: primary-only, approved secondary sectors, or exact product evidence independent of sector.
6. Decide if any high-risk sector is excluded entirely from the first local pilot.
7. Define merchant-facing policy reason granularity without exposing fraud detection internals.

## 9. Recommendation

Use Merchant Taxonomy only to route eligibility and review. Start with a narrow owner-approved intersection of merchant sectors and product types. Never let sector self-selection create product authority, regulated permission, or ranking advantage.
